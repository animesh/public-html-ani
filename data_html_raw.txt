<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
For those due to some unexplainable obstacle or impediment  who  are  unable
to reflect on the profound and sublime teachings of Srimad Bhagavad-Gita  in
its entirety, the Supreme Lord Krishna will reveal  the  essence  of  Srimad
Bhagavad-Gita in verses 65 and  66.  Although  confidential  teachings  have
been collectively revealed throughout the preceding 17  chapters.  Now  Lord
Krishna  will  reveal  the  most  concise  form  of   the   teachings   that
consolidates  them  all.   Graciously  He  commands  <i>sarva-guhytamam  bhuyah</i>
<i>srnu</i> meaning: Hear again My most confidential teachings.  But  now  He  will
bequeath the consummate  conclusion  of  all  conclusions  in  their  purest
essence. Why does the Supreme Lord bestow this?  Because  He  considers  the
reality of how dear His devotees existing  in  the  turbulent  and  unstable
material existence are so beloved by Him. For this  reason  He  reveals  the
absolute essential teachings  in  the  next  two  verses  for  the  ultimate
benefit of His loving devotees. Some  anvaya's  interpret  <i>drdham  hitam</i>  as
most beneficial. Others interpret <i>drdhamati</i>  as  of  firm  resolve;  but  we
interpret <i>drdham istah</i> as extremely dear which is how a beloved is.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><font size=2>There is no commentary for this verse.</font>
</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Among the three paths mentioned  in  the  previous  verse:  <i>bhakti  yoga</i>  or
facilitating communion by exclusive loving devotion unto  the  Supreme  Lord
Krishna or any of His authorised  <i>avatars</i>  or  incarnations  and  expansions
according to Vedic scriptures  is  considered  the  most  exalted  and  pre-
eminent and thus it is  the  most  confidential.  Because  Lord  Krishna  is
extremely affectionate to His devotees who have  Him  alone  as  their  sole
refuge, He will mercifully reveal  in  the  next  two  verses  the  absolute
conclusion and final essence  of  the  entire  divine  discourse  of  Srimad
Bhagavad-Gita.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
In order to dissolve the need to  summarise  the  scriptural  texts  of  the
entire Simad Bhagavad-Gita which  is  poignant  and  deep  in  meaning.  The
Supreme Lord Krishna  out  of  magnanimous  compassion  refined  it  to  its
essence with the words <i>sarva guhyataman</i> the most confidential of  all.  This
ultimate essence He will reveal in the next two verses. It may  be  wondered
why is Lord Krishna emphasising that the ultimate essence is  so  much  more
confidential then all the other previous confidential teachings.  It  is  to
illustrate just how dear and precious his beloved  devotees are to  Him.  So
He Himself most benevolently reveals the  essence which is  the  very  heart
and soul of Srimad Bhagavad-Gita for  the  ultimate  benefit  and  perpetual
welfare of His devotees.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 18, verse 64 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
