<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The actual purport of this chapter is now being concluded.  Those  with  the
spiritual eye  of  discriminative  knowledge  can  see  the  difference  and
distinction between the material  <i>ksetra</i>  or  sphere  of  activity  and  the
spiritual <i>ksetrajna</i> or knower of the sphere of activity. Who  by  reflection
and meditation understand  the  path  to  <i>moksa</i>  or  freedom  from  material
existence and its corollary of <i>samsara</i> or the perpetual cycle of  birth  and
death. Such persons attain <i>param</i> pure spiritual existence.
<p>
I bow down to the Supreme Lord Krishna who appeared  as  the  son  of  Nanda
Maharaj in Vrindavan at the end of Dvapara Yuga and who is of the nature  of
<i>ananda</i> or unending bliss. Who eloquently elaborated and concisely  clarified
what is generally misunderstood that is:  The  distinct  difference  between
transitory <i>prakriti</i> the material  substratum  pervading  physical  existence
and the eternal <i>purusa</i> the Supreme Being  who  manifests  as  <i>paramatma</i>  the
Supreme Soul in  each  and  every  <i>jiva</i>  or  embodied  being  everywhere  in
creation and is the source of the <i>brahman</i> or spiritual substratum  pervading
all existence.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
The path of deliverance from <i>prakriti</i> or the material  substratum  pervading
physical existence and <i>moksa</i> or liberation from the material  existence  for
the <i>jivas</i> is achieved by embracing the 20 virtues given in verses  8  to  12
of  this  chapter  beginning  with  the  word  <i>amanitvam</i>  meaning   humilty,
reverence.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Lord Krishna concludes this chapter by  revealing  that  those  who  by  the
vision of knowledge realise the distinction existing between the  <i>ksetra</i>  or
sphere of activity which is the physical body and the  <i>ksetrajna</i>  or  knower
of the sphere of activity which for the omnipresent  <i>paramatma</i>  the  Supreme
Soul includes each and every the <i>atma</i> or immortal soul and in  the  case  of
the <i>atma</i> is the physical body. Along with those who imbibe and  realise  the
20 virtues given in verses 8 to 12 of this chapter beginning with  <i>amanitvam</i>
or humility, reverence which are the way and means of effecting escape  from
the degradation of mandated corporeal existence in <i>samsara</i> or the  perpetual
cycle of birth and death. Such persons realising their <i>atma</i>  achieve  <i>moksam</i>
or  liberation  from  material  existence  and  attain  <i>param</i>  the   supreme
transcendence.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna concludes this chapter with a brief synopsis  of  what  He  has
elaborated upon distinguishing the <i>ksetra</i> or  sphere  of  activity  and  the
<i>ksetrajna</i> or the  knower  of  the  sphere  of  activity.  The  <i>atma</i>  or  the
individual immortal soul and <i>paramatma</i> or the  all  pervading  Supreme  Soul
and the difference of both from each other as well as from <i>prakriti</i>  or  the
material substratum pervading physical existence. He  proclaims  that  those
by the vision of knowledge inferring that was acquired by the  teachings  of
the spiritual master from the Vedic scriptures  through  who  realise  these
things achieve perfection and attain the supreme destination.
<p>
I take complete refuge in the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna,  the  omniscient  and
omnipotent Supreme Being, the  controller  of  all.  Who  out  of  causeless
compassion categorically instructed  the  discriminative  knowledge  of  the
<i>ksetra</i> and the <i>ksetrajna</i>.
<p>
