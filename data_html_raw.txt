<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
One may wonder do not all humans recognise the <i>atma</i> or immortal soul  within
as  distinct  and  different  from  the  physical  body?  Lord  Krishna   is
addressing such a query here. The bewilderd and  deluded  do  not  recognise
the <i>atma</i> residing within the etheric heart of their  own  physical  body  is
experiencing <i>prakriti,</i> the material substratum pervading physical  existence
through the <i>gunas,</i> the three modes  of  material  nature  by  utilising  the
senses. Hence they are incapable of perceiving the <i>atma</i> departing  from  the
physical body and likewise cannot perceive that the <i>atma</i>  exists equally  in
all <i>jivas</i> as  well.  But  Lord  Krishna  confirms  that  those  realised  in
spiritual intelligence can perceive the <i>atma.</i>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
How one does not perceive and how one is able to  perceive  is  examined  by
Lord Krishna.
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The word <i>vimudha</i> used by Lord Krishna means the ignorant, the  fools,  those
who misconceive the outer, corporeal body to be the <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul
and who do not perceive that the <i>atma</i> is conjoined  with  matter,  bound  to
limited conditions of space and time in various forms of existence as  <i>jivas</i>
or embodied beings exist. Such bewildered <i>jivas</i> never can detemine  how  the
<i>atma</i> enters or how it  departs  with  an  embodied  being.  Neither  can  it
ascertain how although abiding within it is distinctly  different  from  the
<i>jiva</i> nor understand how  the  <i>atma</i>  is  experiencing  that  which  the  <i>jiva</i>
enjoys. Yet those who are <i>jnana-caksusah</i> or endowed  with  spiritual  wisdom
and  enlightened  realisation  can  perceive  the  <i>atma</i>  existing   in   its
essential nature within their own  etheric  heart  and  within  the  etheric
heart of every <i>jiva.</i>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Those who are ignorant and unevolved are not able  to  discriminate  between
the <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul  and  the  physical  body.  Hence  lacking  the
spiritual intelligence they are completely oblivious  of  the  reality  that
the <i>atma</i> is distinctly different from the body and the  senses  even  though
it is experiencing the  senses  when  dwelling  within  the  body,  or  when
transmigrating from the body or when entering a new body as  human,  demigod
or animal due to the influence of the  <i>gunas</i>  or  three  modes  of  material
nature and the resultant tribulation of <i>karma</i> or reactions to  actions.  But
those with spiritual intelligence, possessing the  eye  of  wisdom  acquired
through devotion and knowledge of the Vedic  scriptures  as  taught  by  the
Vaisnava spiritual preceptor will naturally realise both  the  eternal  <i>atma</i>
and the transcendental  Supreme  Lord  in  reality  as  having  no  material
qualities in any respect.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 15, verse 10 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
