<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
If one is not able to perform steady practice in attempting to remember  Him
with  regularity  then  one  should  be  solely  dedicated  to  rituals  and
ceremonies that please the Supreme Lord Krishna such  as  fasting  from  all
grains on <i>ekadasi</i> which is the 11th day of the waxing moon and the 11th  day
of the waning moon. Celebrating on His appearance day of Janmastami  in  the
month  of   <i>Kanya</i>  or  Augest  -September  with  pomp  and   splendour   and
celebrating the  appearance  days  of  all  of  His  <i>avat?rs</i>  or  authorised
incarnations and expansions such as Rama or Narasimhadeva  or  Vamana  which
occur throughout the year. By going on pilgrimage  to  the  holy  places  in
India where the Supreme Lord and His  <i>avat?rs</i>  exclusively  performed  Their
pastimes on Earth. Also  by  performing  <i>sankirtana</i>  or  the  congregational
chanting of Lord Krishna's holy  names.  All  these  activities  please  the
Supreme Lord very much and by performing  them  for  His  satisfaction  will
award liberation to the sincere aspirant if they have accepted  a  spiritual
master in disciplic succession from one of the four authorised  <i>samprad?ya's</i>
as revealed in Vedic scriptures.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
1 Bhagavad-Gita
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
If  one  is  still  unable  to  constantly  endeavour  to  perform  repeated
concentration upon Lord Krishna then one should  at  least  try  to  perform
activities  in  His  service  whenever  possible  such  as  cow  protection,
construction of Vedic temples to house His deity forms,  maintaining  flower
gardens for His worship, cultivation of fruit and vegetable gardens for  His
service, etc. Also in included in these  activities  is  visiting  His  holy
pastime places and hearing about  them,  perambulating  His  holy  <i>dhama</i>  or
abodes in India, prostrating before Him, singing and  dancing  in  glory  to
Him, etc. All these activities for Lord Krishna  should  be  performed  with
causeless,  ardent  affection  in  <i>bhakti</i>  or  exclusive  loving   devotion.
Performing such activities exclusively for the satisfaction of  the  Supreme
Lord prepares the mind for constant remembrance and subsequent  fixed  focus
on Him without deviation.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
If one is unable to even consistently endeavour  to  remember  Lord  Krishna
while restraining the mind from pursuing the objects of the senses  then  He
offers an easier method which is to  perform  activities  dedicated  to  Him
such as chanting His holy names, singing hymns of praise to  Him  alone  and
with others, constructing temples for His deity  forms,  cultivating  Tulasi
gardens for His worship, growing flowers and  vegetables  for  His  service,
cow protection, offering <i>dhupa</i> or incense, <i>dhipa</i> or  ghee  lamps,  <i>puspa</i>  or
flowers, circumambulation of His holy pastime places and <i>dhama's</i> or  abodes,
etc. By perfoming  such  actions  for  the  exclusive  satisfaction  of  the
Supreme Lord Krishna one's mind will become steady and soon be able  to  fix
its focus exclusively upon the Supreme Lord and  subsequent  realisation  of
Him will manifest within the heart.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 12, verse 10 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
