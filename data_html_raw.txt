<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Being so requested the Supreme Lord Krishna proceeds to advise Arjuna  about
what His omnipotent <i>visvarupa</i> or divine universal form  will  be  exhibiting
and inferring that he should pay attention. The  word  <i>pasya</i>  means  behold.
Although  the  form  is  one  it  has  unlimited  features  and  innumerable
different personalities are manifested within it. The  word  <i>rupani</i>  meaning
forms  is  plural  and  denotes  various,  divine,  supernatural  forms   of
variegated types and multicoloured hues such as black,  blue,  yellow,  red,
green and white as well as particular  and  specific  arrangements  of  many
diverse parts and unique shapes of divine expansions harmoniously  connected
in the <i>sataso'tha sahasrasah</i> or hundreds of thousands infinite in number.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna is instructing to behold His all encompassing,  all  sustaining
transcendental <i>visvarupa</i> or divine universal form in hundreds  of  thousands
of unique transcendental  manifestations.  The  word  <i>d?vy?ni</i>  means  divine
denoting non-material spiritual colours and forms never seen before.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The Supreme Lord Krishna being  requested  by  His  surrendered  devotee  to
reveal His <i>visvarupa</i> or divine  universal  form;  responded  with  the  word
<i>pasya</i> meaning behold, His hundred  of  thousands  of  radiant  and  dazzling
transcendental manifestations inferring that he should be  prepared  because
it is difficult to look at. He addresses Arjuna as Partha referring  to  him
as the son of His female devotee Prtha  better  known  as  Kunti.  The  word
<i>rupani</i> meaning forms is used to express  the  manifestation  of  innumerable
multifarious and multicoloured divine forms and shapes.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 11, verse 5 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
