<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
After explaining how the evolution of all <i>jivas</i>  or  embodied  beings  along
with their modifications and transformations all arise from <i>prakriti</i> or  the
material substratum pervading physical existence,  Lord  Krishna  elaborates
how the <i>Purusa</i> or eternal manifestation of the Supreme Lord as the  <i>atma</i>  or
immortal soul is <i>hetuh</i> or responsible for determining  one's  transmigratory
existence. The effect of this is the physical body, the cause is  the  means
to experience the dualities such as joy  and  distress  if  the  senses  and
their production is from the <i>gunas</i> or the actions of goodness,  passion  and
ignorance  producing  <i>karma</i>  or  reactions  from  past  actions  making  the
subsequent modifications and transformations.  <i>Prakriti</i> is the cause of  the
position of the physical body as confirmed  by  Kapila  and  Vedavyasa.  The
<i>Purusa</i> manifested as the <i>atma</i> is the  cause  of  the  happiness  and  misery
experienced which are of its own making. Although the inert <i>prakriti</i>  cannot
by itself be  considered  the  cause  it  is  the  source  of  the  physical
mnaifestation. Neither can the  <i>Purusa</i>  which  is  immortal  and  changeless
experience anything; but inexplicably it happens to both and  by  agency  of
its auspices it is accomplished. The agency referred  to  here  is  that  of
<i>adrishta</i> or the cumulative  results  of  <i>karma</i>  which  acts  as  a  sentient
principle upon even the insentient. The same as fire burns  upwards,  rivers
flow downwards, the wind blows  obliquely  and  milk  from  the  cow  spurts
outwards. Therefore this agency of the insentient <i>p</i>r<i>akriti</i>  is  said  to  be
due to the influence of the <i>Purusa</i> by the experience of  cognition  in  both
joy and misery, happiness and  distress  and  all  such  dualities.  Whereas
these being the qualities of only sentient  beings  the  experience  of  the
<i>Purusa</i> is said to be due to the proximity of <i>prakriti.</i>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The word <i>karyam</i> meaning effect verily signifies the physical body. The  word
<i>karana</i> meaning cause reinforces the reality that the physical  body  is  the
instrument of performing actions. The  word  <i>bhoktrtve</i>  means  experiencing.
Lord Krishna as the <i>purusa</i> a manifestation of the Supreme Lord as  unlimited
consciousness is the cause witnessing and experiencing through all <i>jivas</i>  or
embodied beings. <i>Prakriti</i> or  the  material  substratum  pervading  material
existence is the effect of actions and the  <i>purusa</i>  being  superior  is  the
cause of action.
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
It is <i>prakriti</i> in the form of <i>maya</i> or  the  external  illusory  energy  that
bewilders <i>jivas</i> into believing the physical body  belongs  to  them  and  is
acquired on ones own independently; due to  being  completely  oblivious  of
the reality of Lord Krishna as the sole creator of everything in  all  times
throughout all creation and it is He alone  who  awards  the  power  of  the
<i>jivas</i> to experience the <i>ksetra</i> or field  of  activity  within  the  physical
body and within material existence. Lord Krishna is  known  as  the  Supreme
Controller because everything in exsitence emanates originally from  Him  in
the material worlds and Vaikuntha the  spiritual  worlds.  Even  though  the
Supreme Lord creates everything, to show that the  privilege  of  experience
has been granted to <i>prakriti</i> to a small degree, He uses the word  <i>ucyate</i>  or
speaks in two  places.  The  puport  is  to  emphasize  the  <i>purusa</i>  is  the
principle cause and <i>prakriti</i> is the secondary effect. Specifically  <i>prakriti</i>
is more an instrument of action then experiencing actions.  The  <i>jiva</i>  to  a
lesser extent has been granted agency for experiencing  and  enjoyment   but
the  actual  experiencer  and  enjoyer  is  the  Supreme  Lord  who  is  the
controller of everything and who is known as the <i>purusa</i> and ordains  through
the medium of His localized aspect of <i>paramatma</i> the supreme soul present  in
the etheric heart of all living entities.
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The word <i>karya</i> meaning effect refers to the physical body. The  word  <i>karana</i>
means the instrument of  action  denoting  the  senses  of  perception,  the
organs of action and the mind. In the activation of both  <i>karya</i>  and  <i>karana</i>
to function the <i>atma</i> or immortal soul alone  is  the  cause.  This  confirms
that all manifest activity by which the <i>jiva</i> or embodied  being  experiences
existence is dependent upon <i>prakriti</i> or the  material  substratum  pervading
physical existence which is predicated by the  impulses  of  the  <i>atma.</i>  The
<i>atma</i> merely performs its natural function as monitor  and  director  of  the
<i>jiva</i> in all respects. Affirmation of this is given in  the  following  Vedic
aphorism found in the Vedanta Sutra  II:III.XXXX  beginning  <i>krita  prayatna</i>
<i>apekesah</i> meaning the Supreme Lord induces the <i>atma</i> to act according  to  the
effects experienced by the <i>jiva</i> so that there is  no  contradiction  in  the
prohibitions and injunctions of the Vedic scriptures. The  function  of  the
<i>atma</i> is to witness the <i>karma</i> or reactions to one's actions  and  govern  the
<i>jiva</i> effects of <i>karma</i> as they evolve and grow. The <i>atma</i>  being  embodied  is
the sole cause for a <i>jiva</i> to experience joy and grief through the medium  of
matter. Thus the difference in function in regard to both the <i>atma</i>  and  the
physical body has been briefly elaborated seperately and in conjunction.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The process by which the inherently pure and spiritual <i>atma</i>  which  is  most
blessed comes to experience pleasures and pain  derived  from  contact  with
products of matter is through the  three  <i>gunas</i>  being  modes  of  goodness,
passion and  ignorance  which  are  effects  of  <i>prakriti</i>  and  figuratively
symbolise all derivatives of matter as all matter is within  the  purvey  of
the three gunas. Here in this verse the word  <i>purusa</i>  is  referring  to  His
expansion as the <i>atma</i>  within the heart of all living entities which by  its
own intrinsic nature is liberated and  blissful  but  by  the  influence  of
<i>karma</i> and  <i>prakriti</i>  is  entangled  in  the  material  existence  undergoing
experiences of enjoyment and suffering  based  on  this  relationship  which
manifest according to the dicitates and constraints of   the  adventitiously
and contrary circumstances of past actions from which  arise  effects  known
as <i>karma.</i> The  word  <i>bhoktrtve</i>  meaning  the  experience  of  happiness  and
distress confirms this.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna having explained the evolutionary cycle of material nature,  He
explains of the respective functions of matter  and  spirit  as  they  exist
conjointly.  The  words  <i>karya-karana-kartrtve</i>  mean  the  results  of   the
combination of the evolution of the bodily  organs  and  functions  and  the
influence of the presiding demigods on  the  senses  which  when  coming  in
contact with matter are the cause of the <i>jiva</i> or  embodied  soul  to  be  in
bondage experiencing the consequence of their <i>karma</i>  or  reactions  to  good
and bad actions  and  obliged  to  accept  happiness  and  misery.  This  is
unavoidable as long as one remains  infatuated  and  attracted  to  material
existence.
<p>
