<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Of  letters  of  the   alphabet,   Lord   Krishna's   <i>vibhuti</i>   or   divine,
transcendental opulence is the letter A for it representing  all  speech  is
the foremost. The Vedic scriptures declare that the letter A is  all  speech
and takes many various forms when manifested from  different  parts  of  the
body such as throat, nose,  palate,  abdomen  etc.  Of  compound  words  His
<i>vibhuti</i> is the dual compound such  as  Rama-Krishna  which  exemplifies  the
prominence of both. Before in verse 30 He stated that His <i>vibhuti</i>  was  time
in terms of  controller  regarding  the  longevity  of  all  created  beings
lifespan, in the finite sense; but here He reveals another  aspect  of  time
being His  <i>vibhuti</i>  in  the  infinite  sense  as  an  eternal  ever  flowing
constancy. That time determined the duration of a  life  and  is  terminated
when the <i>jiva</i> or embodied being's life is terminated. While this time  flows
uninterrupted as infinite time. Of creators His <i>vibhuti</i> is <i>dhataham visvato-</i>
<i>mukhah</i> meaning the four faced Brahma who faces can see on all sides.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Lord Krishna reveals that of the letters of  the  alphabet  His  <i>vibhuti</i>  or
divine, transcendental opulence is the Sanskrit letter A-kara which  is  the
root of all other letters.  The  Rig  Veda,   III.II.III  in  the  Aitareya-
Aranyaka section beginning <i>akaro vai sarva-vak</i>  states  that  the  letter  A
itself contains all  speech.  An  example  of  His  <i>vibhuti</i>  of  <i>dvandva</i>  or
compound words are Rama-Krishna. <i>Samasikasya</i> is the class of  compound  word
forming in Sanskrit. Of the several ways to compound  Lord  Krishna  is  the
dual compound of which both terms  are  substanative   attributes  of  equal
position with each other grammatically. His <i>vibhuti</i>  is  also  expressed  as
ever flowing  time  composed  of  uninterupted  divisions  such  as  <i>kala</i>  a
measurement of 8 seconds and <i>muhurta</i> a period  comprising  1/60th  of  a  24
hour day. Among secondary creators His <i>vibhuti</i> is the four  headed  <i>dhataham</i>
known as Brahma.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Among the sounds of speech from the various letters of  all  languages  Lord
Krishna's  <i>vibhuti</i>  or  divine,  transcendental  opulence   is   the   sound
represented by the first letter of the alphabet known as A. Among  different
types of compounds in grammar His <i>vibhuti</i> is the dual  compound  whose  both
parts are complimentary yet independent. Among subjugators  His  <i>vibhuti</i>  is
all subjugating time terminating all existence in the form of Shiva  at  the
time of universal destruction and of creators His <i>vibhuti</i> is  <i>visvato-mukhah</i>
the four- headed Brahma. The Svetara Upanisad VI.XVI states: He who  is  the
knower and devourer of time the omniscient  one  possessing  all  auspicious
attributes etc. In the Udyoga Parva of Mahabharta it  states:  Lord  Krishna
incessantly revolves the wheel of time, the wheel  of  the  worlds  and  the
wheel of all dimensions by His <i>vibhuti.</i> The Supreme Lord Krishna rules  over
all time, all death and all that is moving and non-moving. This  is  eternal
truth.
<p>
