<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Without devotion to  the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna,  transcendental  knowledge
about Him  is  impossible  to  assimilate,  understand  or  intuit.  Amongst
innumerable living beings specifically means  humans  as  all  other  beings
have neither the inclination or the  qualification for <i>moksa</i>  or  liberation
from the material existence. Yet among hundreds of thousands of humans  only
one due to great merit ever endeavours for <i>moksa</i> and among the  hundreds  of
thousands who endeavour for <i>moksa</i> perchance one  will  actually  achieve  it
due to extraordinary activities from a previous birth and  of  the  hundreds
of thousands who actually achieve <i>moksa</i> and reach perfection perchance  only
one by the mercy of the Supreme Lord will know  Him  in  reality.  Yet  even
though this transcendental knowledge is very confidential Lord Krishna  will
reveal it to His devotee. This is the purport.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Extremely rare  is  transcendental  knowledge  regarding  the  Supreme  Lord
Krishna received.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Hari OM! The paramount nature of the Supreme Lord  is  specially  emphasised
in this verse. Of innumerable living  entities  existing  in  creation  less
then one-tenth of 1% attempt to endeavour for perfection. Of  the  minuscule
amount of living entities  who  do  endeavour  less  then  one-tenth  of  1%
percent actually achieve perfection.  The  Padma  Purana  states  that  even
among those perfected only a few such as Brahma and  Shiva  actually  really
know the Supreme Lord is Krishna. As far as others are  concerned  knowledge
alone  of  Lord  Krishna  is  sufficient  enough  to  grant  them  <i>moksa</i>  or
liberation from the cycle of birth and death in the material  existence.  In
the Bhagavat Purana it states that   even  among  100,000  liberated  beings
only less then one-tenth of 1% factually comprehend  that  Lord  Krishna  is
the ultimate creator of all. Every being who has achieved <i>moksa</i> in the  past
and those who are  achieving <i>moksa</i> in  the  present  all  without  exception
have been and are devotees of the Supreme Lord  Krishna  and  His  Vedically
authorised incarnations. This also applies as well to all  beings  who  will
achieve <i>moksa</i> in the future. Amongst all these eternal devotees  Brahma  the
architect of all beings in the material creation is the  foremost  and  thus
it has been stated in the Sat Tattva text that the devotees of Lord  Krishna
are the very best of all living entities.
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Humans are designated to act in  accordance  with  the  injunctions  of  the
Vedic scriptures.  Thousands may be found that adhere to the injunctions  of
the Vedas but hard to find is one who adheres and perseveres until the  goal
is reached and perfection is achieved. Of thousands who may  be  found  that
fortunately have sustained their efforts and are persevering until the  goal
is reached and perfection is  achieved  there  is  scarcely  one  discovered
among them who knows the Supreme position of Lord  Krishna  and  offers  all
their activities to Him. And of thousands who may know His supreme  position
it is extremely rare to find one who knows Lord Krishna as He really is  and
offers themselves in loving devotion to Him.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna is speaking in this way to emphasise that the knowledge  He  is
prepared to reveal is very rarely achieved and difficult to acquire. Out  of
innumerable, countless souls some have attained  human  status  and  amongst
them hardly one in thousands of humans is qualified in Vedic scriptures  and
strives  for  <i>atma  tattva</i>  or  realisation  of  the  soul.  Of  those   few
endeavouring for <i>atma tattva</i> hardly one in thousands  actually  achieves  it
and out of thousands who after having  achieved  <i>atma  tattva</i>e  and  finally
attained <i>moksa</i> or liberation  from  the  material  existence,  amongst  this
group some very rare personalities are  actually  aware  of  Lord  Krishna's
paramount position and divine glories in reality. This  is  because  without
<i>bhakti</i> or loving devotion unto Lord Krishna it is not possible to  know  and
perceive Him. This Lord  Krishna  confirms  Himself  at  the  conclusion  of
chapter eighteen, verse fifty-five  when  He  declares,  That  only  through
devotion does one come to the platform of communion with Him.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 7, verse 3 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
