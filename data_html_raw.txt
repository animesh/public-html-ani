<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna explains  that  the  perceptions  of  the  demoniac  stem  from
exceedingly   impure  minds  and  deranged  intellects  that  are  extremely
limited and so materialistically orientated  that  they  are  only  able  to
accept and believe what they  can   directly  perceive  by  their  mind  and
senses. They incorrigibly perform heinous and horrible deeds  that  lead  to
the degradation and destruction of all creation.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Continuing further Lord Krishna gives some precise insights to the  demoniac
mentality.
<i>1) Avastabhya</i> means indiscriminately adopting erroneously as in the verse
before.
<i>2) Nasta-atmanah</i> means devoid of any contact with their immortal soul. 
<i>3) Alpa-buddhaya</i> means limited intellect due to being incognizant of  the
cognizant.
<i>4) Ugra-karmanah</i> means vile, heinous, horrendous  activities  destructive
to all.
<p>
Such  creatures  are  demoniac  and  are   a   parasitic,   virulent   virus
destabilising all creation.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna confirms that the demoniac embracing the  doctrine  of  atheism
negate their own <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul  because  of  illusion  and  their
inability to accept anything other than the direct perception of  what  they
experience with their mind and  senses.  Hence  life  after  life  they  are
oblivious to the eternal spiritual position inherent within  them  and  they
become deluded lost souls. The conceptions they  adhere  to  are  fallacious
and  their  activities  are  despicable  because  they  lack  discriminative
knowledge of the difference between the <i>atma</i> and the physical body. What  is
eternal and what is not eternal. What should be accepted and what should  be
rejected. Thus they are evilly disposed, enemies of all, full  of  violence,
devoted to  diabolical  actions  and  the  root  of  chaos  and  destruction
throughout creation.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 16, verse 9 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
