<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now the six properties of the demoniac nature are concisely  given  here  by
Lord Krishna.  <i>Dambha</i>  is  ostentation  which  arises  from  belief  in  the
sanctimony  of  perverting  religion.  <i>Darpha</i>  is  pride  due  to  erroneous
conceptions of what is privileged birth. <i>Abhimanah</i>  is  arrogance  which  is
conceit and vanity due to material  delusions  of  education,  position  and
wealth.  <i>Parusyam</i> is harshness and cruelty devoid of mercy. <i>Krodha</i> is  anger
exemplified by total lack of control of the mind  due  to  frustrated  greed
and  lust.  <i>Ajnanam</i>  is  ignorance  and  the   absence   of   knowledge   in
discriminating what is eternal and what is temporary, what is righteous  and
what  is  unrighteous.  All  the  demoniac  properties  are  accompanied  by
fiendishness which is also a component of the demoniac and exists  in  those
born in the demoniac nature.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Lord Krishna gives the six demerits that are part  of  the  demoniac  nature
and  are  formidable  obstacles  obstructing  all  progress   in   spiritual
advancement. The word <i>damah</i> or  ostentatious  denotes  hypocrisy,  mendacity
and  lack  of  religiosity.  <i>Darpah</i>  is  pride  erroneously   exuding   from
intoxication of position, wealth and power.  The  word  <i>krodah</i>  means  anger
resulting from frustration causing animosity and the impetus to  injure  and
harm others by <i>parusyam</i> or cruelty and harshness without any mercy. This  is
a defect especially found in  sovereigns  and  rulers  due  to  <i>abhimanh</i>  or
extreme egotism in  the  illusion  that  they  are  the  most  powerful  and
worshipable instead of the Supreme Lord. Thus their mentality being  deluded
is  <i>ajnanam</i>  or  ignorance,  void  of  actual  knowledge  and  destitute  in
discrimination of what is eternal and what is temporary. 
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now Lord Krishna discloses the six  properties  predominating  the  demoniac
nature.
<p>
<p>
<i>dambah</i> is ostentation or a charade of pretentious  actions  perverting
religion.
<p>
<p>
<i>darpa</i> is pride or the unnatural  proclivity  for  excess  experiencing
sense objects.
<p>
<p>
<i>abhimanah</i> is arrogance or egotism concerning ones  self  interest  and
importance
<p>
<p>
<i>krodah</i> is anger which arises  when  greed  and  lust  is  impeded  and
frustrated.
<p>
<p>
<i>parusyam</i> is belligerence  or  spurious  indignation  towards  what  is
righteous.
<p>
<p>
<i>ajananam</i> is ignorance and lack of discrimination of  the  eternal  and
the temporal.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Those born in the demoniac nature act contrary to all that is righteous  and
fiendishly oppose the ordinances and injunctions  of  the  Vedic  scriptures
beneficial for all creation.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Having revealed the divine qualities the Supreme Lord Krishna discloses  the
six demoniac properties which are  to  be  rejected  and  abandoned  in  all
situations. <i>Dambah</i> is ostentation or hypocrisy  by  pretentiously  parodying
righteousness. <i>Darpah</i> is pride caused by exaggerated egotism  due  to  one's
birth. <i>Abhinamah</i> is arrogance  due  to    education,  position  and  wealth.
<i>Krodah</i> is anger resulting from frustration of greed and lust and causes  one
to give the burning pain one feels to others. <i>Parusyam</i>  is  harshness,  lack
of kindness and merciless opposition to that which is righteous. <i>Ajnanam</i>  is
ignorance  in  determining  what  is  eternal  and  what  is  temporal   and
subsequently the inability to discriminate between  what  is  righteous  and
beneficial and what is unrighteous and harmful to one's own  existence.  The
copulative  <i>ca</i>  meaning  and,  includes  the  vices  such  as  fiendishness,
perversion, lust, greed, etc.
<p>
Thus the demoniac oppose  even  the  injunctions  and  prohibitions  of  the
eternal Vedic scriptures while concocting,  hypothecating  and  engaging  in
heinous activities that erode the eternal values of  righteousness  for  all
living beings in their  fiendish  attempt  to  degrade  and  degenerate  all
creation.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 16, verse 4 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
