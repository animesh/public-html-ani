<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Being thus humbly entreated, Lord Krishna consoles Arjuna and cheers him  up
with the word <i>prasannena</i> which means being pleased and explains  to  him  in
this verse and the next two that there is no need  for  him  to  be  fearful
when  out  of  His  grace  he  has  been  shown  the  resplendent,  supreme,
unlimited, all pervading and powerful <i>visvarupa</i> or divine universal form  by
virtue of His phenomenal internal potency known as Yogamaya which was  never
before seen by anyone else previously.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The Padma Purana states that the Supreme Lord Krishna is known as <i>visvam</i>  or
all pervasive because of  the  maximum  fullness  and  completeness  of  His
attributes. The <i>visvarupa</i> or divine universal form had not been seen  before
on Earth previous to Arjuna, the son of  Indra  seeing  it.  This  does  not
preclude that it had never been seen anywhere else in earlier  ages  but  at
no other time did His <i>visvarupa</i>  exhibit unlimitedly as much as  Arjuna  had
witnessed. The Brahmanda Purana states that  in  previous  ages  in  various
planetary systems the <i>visvarupa</i> had been seen by Brahma and  then  after  by
Siva and later it had been seen by Indra.  Therefore  others  had  witnessed
the <i>visvarupa</i> in special situations but Arjuna was the first to  see  it  on
Earth.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna magnanimously expressed because He was prasannena tavarjunadam
meaning very pleased by Arjuna and thus exhibited His visvarupa or divine
universal form. The word tejo-mayam means resplendent and glorious, visvam
means all pervasive, anantam means infinite without beginning, middle or
end. The word adyam means first, the original, He who was before and prior
to all else and the words atma-yogat means by His own infallible free will
which manifests as His sakti or internal energy known as Yogamaya. Arjuna
was the Supreme Lords devotee who by His grace bequeathed upon him the
vision to see the visvarupa which had never been revealed on Earth before.
Next will be explained that except by bhakti or exclusive loving devotion
it is not possible by any means whatsoever to realise the Supreme Lord
Krishna and attain communion with Him.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Being thus appeased Lord Krishna tells Arjuna He is pleased with him and
asks why is he feeling trepidation after requesting to witness His
visvarupa or divine universal form which was revealed to bless him. The
visvarupa of infinite potency and unlimited splendour is the prime cause of
creation and never before seen by anyone else on Earth.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 11, verse 47 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
