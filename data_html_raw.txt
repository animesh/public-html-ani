<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The three fold divisions of <i>danam</i> or charity are  now  being  delineated  by
Lord Krishna. To give in charity to worthy recipients is a duty.  Those  who
give such charity without  the  slightest  inclination  for  any  reciprocal
return are those  situated  in  <i>sattva  guna</i>  the  mode  of  goodness.  Such
aspirants will offer <i>danam</i> at an auspicious place such as  Kuruksetra  where
Lord Krishna spoke the Bhagavad-Gita or anywhere along the bank of the  2000
miles long sacred river Ganga. They will offer <i>danam</i> at an  auspicious  time
such as during a solar eclipse or the holy  day  of  <i>ekadasi</i>  the  11th  day
preceding the new  moon  and  the  full  moon.  They  will  offer  <i>danam</i>  to
auspicious personalities such as Vaisnava brahmanas endowed with  wisdom  in
knowing the conclusions of the Vedic scriptures or they will offer <i>danam</i>  to
a temple of the Supreme Lord Krishna or any of His  authorised  incarnations
as revealed in Vedic scriptures. These are  all  <i>danam</i>  situated  in  <i>sattva</i>
<i>guna.</i> The word <i>patre</i> means qualified person, it can also mean  protector  if
analysed from its root <i>pa.</i> Who is better to protect one  then  the  Vaisnava
brahmanas who can reveal the conclusive truths of the Vedic  scriptures  and
save one from <i>samsara</i> the perpetual cycle of birth and death.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
It is a duty to offer <i>danam</i> or charity to worthy  recepients.  Lord  Krishna
explains that <i>danam</i> that is offered without  expectation  of  reward,  hopes
for services or wishing anything in return that is offered at an  auspicious
place, at an auspicious time to spiritually enlightened Vaisnavas  brahmanas
steeped in the conclusive  knowledge  of  the  Vedic  scriptures  is  to  be
understood as the best of <i>danam</i> and situated in  <i>sattva  guna</i>  the  mode  of
goodness.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna describes the threefold divisions of <i>danam</i> or  charity.  It  is
an act of duty to give <i>danam</i> accordingly  to  one's  capability.  The  <i>danam</i>
that is offered freely from the heart to Vaisnavas  and   brahmanas  without
expectation of receiving anything back in return,  that  is  bestowed  in  a
holy place like Navadwip Dham or Jagannatha Puri, that is bequeathed  at  an
auspicious time such as the month of Kartika or the month of Magha is  <i>danam</i>
situated in <i>sattva guna</i> the mode of goodness.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 17, verse 20 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
