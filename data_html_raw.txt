<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
This verse reveals that one should repeatedly vibrate  the  sacred  syllable
<i>OM</i> the personalised sound vibration  of  the  Supreme  Lord  which  is  non-
different from the <i>brahman</i> or spiritual substratum pervading all  existence;
both being different aspects of the Supreme, and think of Lord  Krishna  who
is manifest by expressing the vibration of <i>OM.</i> One who  departs  their  life
in this way at the moment death attains the  eternal,  ultimate  destination
and attains the Supreme Lord who is one without a second.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Except through the <i>Brahmanadi</i> which is one of the 100 nerve centers  in  the
crown of the head; if the life force of a <i>jiva</i> or living  entity  exits  the
body by any of the other 99 nerve centers  it  gets  diverted  and  recycled
back into <i>samsara</i> the perpetual cycle of birth and death  without  attaining
<i>moksa</i> or liberation. Both the Vedic scriptures Vyasa Yoga and  Moksa  Dharma
are specify that when <i>jivas</i> exits through the eyes they go to the sun,  when
they exit  through the ears or any of the 99 centers they go  to  the  place
that corresponds with it but they all remain in the  physical  manifestation
and do not lead to the spiritual plane as does the <i>Brahmanadi.</i>The word <i>hrdi-</i>
<i>nirudhya</i> means confined to the heart and denotes meditating on the  <i>atma</i>  or
soul and the Supreme Lord Krishna.  The  Padma  Purana  states:  Because  He
attracts He is in the  hearts  of  all  <i>jivas</i>  and  attracts  them   towards
Himself, He is known as <i>Hrdi</i> the heart. When the life force is stationed  in
the head it is not possible to abide in the heart.  The  Vyas  Yoga  states:
Wherever the life force abides that is where the  <i>jiva</i>  and  the  mind  also
abide. Thus only the one who has prepared themselves throughout their  lives
in proper mediation is able to concentrate in equanimity and is  fully  able
to be in equanimity. This is the purport.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
As  the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna  is  symbolised  by  the  one  word  <i>OM</i>  His
personalised  transcendental  sound  vibration,  the  <i>brahman</i>  or  spiritual
substratum pervading all existence is also known by the one word <i>OM.</i>
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Systematically vibrating the singular syllable <i>OM</i> vibrated as A-U-M.  It  is
the personal sign, symbol and sound of the <i>aksaram</i> or  imperishable.  <i>OM</i>  is
the indicator and Lord  Krishna  is  the  indicated  Who  is  to  be  to  be
profoundly and humbly meditated upon. Whomsoever departs from this  life  at
the moment of death fixing the <i>prana</i> or life force in  the  crown  of  their
head in the manner so indicated  goes  to  the  exalted,  eternal  spiritual
worlds. The words <i>paramam gatim</i> mean  the  supreme  destination  from  where
there is no return granting <i>moksa</i> or liberation and includes <i>atma tattva</i>  or
realisation of the soul which is in essence comparable to the  Supreme  Lord
and contra distinguished from matter and everything material. More  evidence
of this will be given later in this chapter in verses  20  and  21.  So  the
method of meditation for those who are <i>atharthi</i> or seekers  of  fortune  and
for those who are <i>jijnansur</i> or those aspiring realisation of  the  soul  has
been duly given being similar. Next the method of divine  meditation  suited
for the <i>jnani</i> or the aspirant for the Supreme Lord and the nature  of  their
goal will be revealed.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
One who chants the sacred syllable <i>OM</i> which is the personal sound  vibration
of the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna  and  which  expresses  the  <i>brahman</i>  or  the
spiritual substratum pervading all existence, constantly meditating on  Lord
Krishna who is the full  absolute  aspect  of  the  <i>brahman</i>  and  who  while
vacating their physical body at the moment  of  death  departs  through  the
<i>sushanna</i> channel situated in the crown of  the  head  reaches  according  to
qualification and gradation of one's lifetime practice, attains the  eternal
spiritual plane described in the Vedic scriptures known as the Upanisads.
<p>
