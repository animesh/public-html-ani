<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Verily the hosts of demigods feeling aggrieved are entering  Lord  Krishna's
<i>visvarupa</i> taking refuge.  The  liberated  sages  and  perfected  beings  are
offering superb prayers praising the Supreme Lords glories with  <i>svasti  iti</i>
<i>uktva</i> meaning all auspiciousness throughout creation.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The demigods being exclusively from <i>sattva-guna</i> or the  nature  of  goodness
rejoice upon seeing Lord Krishna's <i>visvarupa</i> or divine  universal  form  and
are awe inspired and enraptured. Some among  them  were  frightened  by  the
<i>visvarupa</i> terrifying aspect which was also wonderful and bowed  down  to  it
with folded hands offering eulogies of praise and  prayers  of  supplication
each  according  to  their  perceptions.  Other  great  souls  such  as  the
<i>Siddhas</i> or perfected beings who have realised the higher  and  lower  truths
utter the benediction <i>svastity</i> meaning my there be all  auspiciousness.  The
<i>maharishis</i> or liberated sages upon beholding the  <i>visvarupa</i>  loudly  resound
the words <i>Jaya Jaya</i> meaning all glory, all glory and  spontaneously  compose
wonderful Sankrit hymns in glorification of it while   performing  <i>sankirtan</i>
or congregational singing of the Supreme  Lords  Krishna's  <i>visvarupa</i>  along
with His transcendental names, forms, qualities and pastimes.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The descriptions of the afflictions which were experienced  is  iterated  in
this verse and the next two. All creation was greatly alarmed by  the  fiery
all pervading aspect of Lord Krishna's <i>visvarupa</i> or  divine  universal  form
which  appeared  terrifying  and  frightening.  The  demigods  being  direct
servitors of the Supreme Lord  functioning  in  the  capacity  of  universal
management approached Him for shelter while others of them being  frightened
stayed  a  far  distance  and  with  folded   palms   offered   prayers   of
supplication. Hosts of exalted sages and perfected  beings  could  be  heard
shouting <i>Jaya! Jaya!</i> All glory! All glory! Observing the unlimited faces  of
the <i>visvarupa</i> with their mouths emitting blazing fire like  the  devastating
inferno  at  the  time  of   universal   destruction.   Benevolently   these
enlightened  and  liberated  sages  chanted  <i>svastity</i>  meaning  may  all  be
auspicious and extolled the Supreme Lord  with  excellent  hymns  describing
His greatness and choice prayers praising  His  glories  entreating  Him  to
protect all creation.
<p>
