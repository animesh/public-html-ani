<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The result of <i>yoga</i> or the science of the individual consciousness  attaining
communion with the ultimate consciousness verifies that the <i>yogi</i> is  greater
than the ascetics who perform  ritualistic  penance's  like  <i>Chandrayana</i>  or
fasting with the cycles of  the  moon,  the  <i>jnani's</i>  or  knowers  of  Vedic
scripture without the requisite realisation and the  <i>karmi's</i>  or  those  who
perform prescribed Vedic activities for fruitive results  and  rewards  such
as <i>Ishta's</i> or rites and rituals for attaining material gains in the  present
life or in <i>svarga loka</i> the heavenly spheres in the afterlife  and  <i>Purta</i>  or
acts of charity such as well digging for public use, giving food in  charity
etc. Therefore Lord Krishna is encouraging Arjuna to be a yogi.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><font size=2>There is no commentary for this verse.</font>
</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
As <i>yoga</i> or the science of the individual consciousness  attaining  communion
with the ultimate consciouness leads to  the  highest  aspiration  of  human
existence. It is known that <i>yoga</i> is greater what is attainable  by  ascetics
living austerities, greater than what is attainable by Vedic knowledge  that
is bereft of knowledge of the soul and greater than what is attained by  the
performance of ritualistic ceremonies prescribed  in  the  Vedas  concerning
fruitive rewards. Hence the  <i>yogi</i>  is  superior  to  all  these.  Thus  Lord
Krishna encourages Arjuna to become a  <i>yogi.</i>  This  verse  is  a  eulogy  on
higher theosophy and it  relates  to  the  acquisition  of  <i>atma  tattva</i>  or
realisation of the soul as the preliminary step to God realisation.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Therefore a person sincerely seeking their eternal well  being  should  rely
on <i>yoga</i> more than any other means for accomplishing the fundamental  purpose
of  human  existence.  The  <i>yogi</i>  or  one  perfecting  the  science  of  the
individual consciousness with the ultimate consciousness is superior to  the
ascetics who practice ritualistic austerities like the  fasting  vows  which
follow the cycle of the moon. The <i>yogi</i> is regarded to be superior  to  those
who are well versed and knowledgeable of the Vedic scriptures and a <i>yogi</i>  is
also superior to those who perform prescribed Vedic activities for  fruitive
results like an <i>Agnihotra</i> or  fire  sacrifice.  Therefore  Lord  Krishna  is
encouraging Arjuna to be a <i>yogi.</i>
<p>
