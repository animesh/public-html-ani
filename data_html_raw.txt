<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Concluding this theme Lord Krishna states that the <i>danam</i> or charity in  <i>tama</i>
<i>guna</i> or the mode of ignorance is that which is  given  at  the  wrong  place
meaning that  which  is  not  revealed  as  holy  or  sacred  in  the  Vedic
scriptures. <i>Danam</i> given at the wrong time is like at  sunset  which  is  the
time  when  ghosts  and  demons  become  active  or  when  the  astrological
calculations are not favourable. <i>Danam</i> given to  unworthy  persons  such  as
those masquerading as spiritual teachers who are not even  conscious  enough
to be vegetarian and other charlatans who pretend they are spiritual but  in
reality they have no knowledge of the <i>atma</i> the immortal soul, they  have  no
knowledge of how to even get themselves out of <i>samsara</i> the  perpetual  cycle
of birth and death  and  have  no  knowledge  of  the  Vedic  scriptures  or
devotion to Lord Krishna. Even if accidently they are in a proper place  and
by chance give <i>danam</i> at a proper time to a worthy recipient. Those  in  <i>tama</i>
<i>guna</i> give it without proper regard and do so without  observing  the  proper
etiquette warranted one so worthy. They will not be  hospitable.  They  will
act haughty and not bow down and offer respects. If they are rich they  will
disdainfully give a large amount of wealth but in a  contemptuous  way  like
they are doing the holy saint a favour. All this is <i>danam</i> situated  in  <i>tama</i>
<i>guna</i>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now Lord Krishna states that <i>danam</i>  or  charity  given  at  an  inauspicious
location such as in cities  where  cows  are  slaughtered,  at  inauspicious
times such as dark of night and to inauspicious persons such as meat  eaters
are situated in <i>tama guna</i> the mode of darkness. Also showing  disrespect  by
failing to properly welcome a worthy recipient such as a  Vaisnava  brahmana
who knows <i>atma tattva</i> or knowledge of the immortal  soul.  As  also  showing
disdain by failing to wash his feet and drink the water,  showing  dishonour
by failing to offer ceremonial  etiquette  by  performing  <i>arati</i>  of  waving
lamps, incense and flowers, etc. are all considered to be in <i>tama  guna</i>  the
mode of ignorance even if accompanied with opulent gifts.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Here Lord Krishna speaks of the <i>danam</i> or charity characterised by <i>tama  guna</i>
the mode of ignorance. The <i>danam</i> which is given at an  improper  place  such
as a  city  where  cows  are  slaughtered  and  inhabited  by  degraded  and
unrighteous people like <i>mlecchas</i> or meat eaters. The <i>danam</i> that is given  at
an improper  time  such  as  the  48  minutes  of  twilight  or  during  the
astrological  influence  of  the  nakshatras  or  stars  <i>Ashlesha,  Jyeshtha</i>
and  <i>Mool.</i>  The  <i>danam</i>  that  is  given  to  unworthy  recipients  such   as
charlatans, fools, pretenders, entertainers, etc.  The  <i>danam</i>  given  in  an
insulting, complacent or pretentious  manner  even  if  given  to  a  worthy
recipient at a proper time and place is spoiled due to  a  lack  of  respect
and regard along with the absence of etiquette in honouring such a  one  and
lack of faith in the absolute authority of the Vedic scriptures and so  must
languish  in  the   mire  of  <i>tama  guna</i>  along  with   the   aforementioned
improprieties.
<p>
