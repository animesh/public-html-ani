<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Of purifiers Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i> or divine,  transcendental  opulence  is
the wind. Of wielders of weapons His  <i>vibhuti</i>  is  Parasurama  who  slew  in
battle all the <i>ksatriyas</i> or warriors class 21 times  with  His  mighty  axe.
Among fish His <i>vibhuti</i> is a particular fish that can swallow a  whole  whale
and lives in the deepest part of the ocean known as <i>Makara</i> and  amongst  all
rivers, Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i> is <i>jahnavi</i> a name of the holy Ganga.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Being of the  form  of  <i>ananda</i>  or  bliss,  being  completely  spiritual  of
Himself, being the  delighter  of  the  people  Lord  Krishna's  <i>vibhuti</i>  or
divine, transcendental opulence is known as Rama. In the  Shandilya  section
it states: Of blissful form, unlimited, from whom the world revels is  known
as Rama. <i>Ra</i> and <i>ama</i> are the root  and  together  as  Rama  mean  exceedingly
pleasing to all.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Lord Krishna reveals that of things that purify  and  things  that  are  the
swiftest His <i>vibhuti</i> or divine, transcendental opulence  is  the  wind.  The
words <i>sastra-bhrtam</i> means weapon wielding heroes of  which  His  <i>vibhuti</i>  is
Rama but unlike other <i>vibhuti</i> which are  indirect.  Rama  is  an  <i>avatar</i>  or
direct incarnation of the Supreme Lord  Krishna  who  manifests  Himself  as
Rama the paragon of weapon wielding heroes. Indirect <i>vibhutis</i>  such  as  the
Maruts or the Vasus are still <i>jivas</i> or embodied  souls  even  though  imbued
with a filament of  the  Supreme  Lords  potency.  In  relationship  to  the
Supreme Lord they are exactly like potencies  serving  Him  as  the  weapons
wielded by Rama are likewise serving Him.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Among purifiers Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i> or  divine,  transcendental  opulence
is the swift moving wind. Among weapon wielding warriors His <i>vibhuti</i> is  the
valiant hero Rama who is a <i>lila</i> <i>avatar</i> or divine pastime incarnation of  the
Supreme Lord Krishna being His catur-vyuya expansion  Vasudeva.  One  should
never think that Rama born of King  Dasaratha  in  the  solar  line  of  the
exalted Raghu dynasty is non-different from the Supreme Lord Krishna  except
in <i>rasa</i> or mood. The word Rama may also be interpreted to be Parasurama  the
son of Jamadagni and  Renuka,  who  was  a  <i>sakyavesa  avatar</i>  or  empowered
incarnation of Lord Krishna who slew all the <i>ksatriyas</i>  21  times  with  His
mighty axe. Among rivers His <i>vibhuti</i> is <i>jahnavi</i> the  daughter  of  the  sage
Jahnu known as the holy river Ganges.
<p>
