<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Having previously declared that transmigratory existence arises due to  lack
of proper  discrimination  between  spirit  and  matter.  Lord  Krishna  now
instructs the difference between the <i>atma</i> or immortal soul and <i>prakriti</i>  the
material substratum pervading physical existence, which by knowing  has  the
potency to terminate <i>samsara</i> or the perpetual cycle of birth and  death.  He
reveals that  one  who  sees  the  Supreme  Lord  in  His  manifestation  of
<i>paramatma</i> the Supreme Soul existing equally within  all  <i>jivas</i>  or  embodied
beings and everything inanimate  as  the  <i>brahman</i>  or  spiritual  substratum
pervading all existence. Such a person understands  that  no  <i>jiva</i>  actually
perishes  when  the  body  perishes  as  every  <i>atma</i>  is  eternal.  Such  an
enlightened seeing equally  perfectly perceives the actual truth of  reality
which others are incapable of recognising.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Lord  Krishna  confirms  with  the  words  <i>pasyati</i>  meaning   perceives   or
discriminates that one who realises  that  <i>paramatma</i>  or  the  Supreme  Soul
existing equally within all <i>jivas</i> or embodied beings  is  the  <i>ksetrajna</i>  or
knower of the sphere of activity and that  it  is  distinctly  distinguished
from <i>prakriti</i> the material substratum  pervading  physical  existence  which
manifests as unlimited forms such as demigod, human, animal, etc.  In  other
words  recognising  the  Supreme  Lord  as  the  same  omnipresent   eternal
consciousness localised within each and every living  entity  and  realising
that it is singular. Also distinguishing the fact that  while  all  physical
bodies decay and perish; the <i>atma</i> or individual immortal soul does not  ever
decay or perish because it has an eternal, transcendental  nature.  This  is
confirmed  in  the  Vishnu   Purana:   VI.VII.LVIII   beginning   <i>pradhanadi</i>
<i>viseshantam</i> meaning: the undecaying within the decaying.  One who  correctly
comprehends the <i>atma</i> in this manner properly perceives reality. But one  who
thinks that the  <i>atma</i>  is  subject  to  birth  and  death  and  that  it  is
modifiable and variable like the forms of living entities which  are  always
changing; then such a person will always remained  confined  in  <i>samsara</i>  or
the perpetual cycle of birth and death in material existence.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Having clarified the reality that all  <i>jivas</i>  or  embodied  beings  entering
into material existence is caused by the combination of spirit  and  matter.
Now Lord Krishna confirms that one who sees the Supreme  Lord  as  <i>paramatma</i>
the  Supreme  Soul  situated  equally  <i>sarvesu  bhutesu</i>  within  all  living
entities within all species of life and who can perceive  the  Supreme  Lord
existing  through  the  <i>brahman</i>  or  spiritual  substratum   pervading   all
existence, also in perishable matter. Such a  person  is  one  who  actually
perceives reality. 
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 13, verse 28 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
