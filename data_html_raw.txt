<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Now the nature of knowledge in <i>raja guna</i> or mode of passion is explained  by
Lord Krishna. The knowledge where one imagines all <i>jivas</i> or embodied  beings
as distinct, possessing different <i>atmas</i> or souls  diversely  conditioned  by
pleasure and happiness and pain and suffering is situated in <i>raja guna.</i> 
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
That knowledge which is contrary to the Vedic scriptures and the  antithesis
to the omnipotent absolute authority of the Supreme Lord  Krishna  is  known
to be situated in <i>raja guna</i> the mode of passion. The words <i>nana-bhavan</i>  mean
multiplicity of natures. Those who are oblivious that the  Supreme  Lord  is
the one underlying reality pervading all creation,  simultaneously  existing
within the etheric heart of every <i>jiva</i> or embodied being in  existence;  are
situated in <i>raja guna.</i> 
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
If one is unaware of the Supreme Lord  Krishna's  unparalleled  position  as
the absolute sovereign ruler of all existence, or is  bewildered  about  His
indomitable, paramount position or thinks that any other  god  can  manifest
creation  or  are   influenced   by   inferior   concepts,   doctrines   and
philosophies concerning creation,  the  <i>jiva</i>  and  the  Supreme  Lord;  such
erroneous and fallacious thinking devoid of a connection to  reality  impels
one to obstinately and belligerently  try  to  justify  and  validate  their
illusory belief systems. Such fools of  miniscule  knowledge  and  misguided
intelligence are unable  to  mount  a  rational  argument  supporting  their
viewpoint and odiously cling to their bogus suppositions and faulty  claims.
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The word <i>prthaktvena</i> means separate individuality  alluding  to  differences
in appearance between  <i>jivas</i>  or  embodied  beings.  The  words  <i>nana-bhavan</i>
refers to plurality of substance in falsely surmising that the  <i>atma</i>  varies
in quality or quantity with the variance of the physical body  inhabited  by
the <i>jiva</i> or to wrongly conclude that the <i>atma</i> varies  in  size  and  potency
according to the body it is  in.  This  mentality  is  indicative  of  those
situated in <i>raja guna</i> the mode of passion and  includes  those  desirous  of
receiving rewards for their actions.   
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
That knowledge which has been characterised  with  <i>raja  guna</i>  the  mode  of
passion cognises the manifold existence of the <i>jiva</i>  or  embodied  being  as
related  to  physical  bodies,  seeing  division  in  difference  of  forms,
species, genre, etc. Establishing criteria based on  bodily  conception  and
oblivious to the true reality of the omnipresent <i>atma</i> or immortal soul  such
<i>jivas</i> are undeniably rooted in <i>raja guna.</i> The particle  <i>tu</i>  meaning  however
indicates that <i>raja guna</i> inferior to <i>sattva guna.</i>
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 18, verse 21 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
