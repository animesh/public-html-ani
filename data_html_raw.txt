<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
At this time a curious puzzlement overcame Arjuna and He humbly became  very
respectful  and  supplicating  himself  before  the  Supreme  Lord   Krishna
confessed that he could not comprehend the inclination of the Supreme  Lords
actions and requests Him to reveal His intentions  regarding  His  <i>visvarupa</i>
because it was impossible to fathom.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Now there is greater curiosity. An enquiry for more details is beseeched  to
understand further who Lord Krishna actually is.  In  normal  existence  one
knows another by their attributes and how one represents themselves but  one
does not know them by their actual inherent nature as it is  not  disclosed.
It is not that Arjuna did not know otherwise he  would  have  not  addressed
Lord Krishna in this verse as <i>Vishnu</i> meaning He who is all pervasive and  in
previous verses as <i>Acutya</i> meaning He who is infallible, <i>Keshava</i>  meaning  He
who controls Brahma and Shiva, <i>Vasudeva</i>  meaning  He  from  whom  everything
emanates, etc. The purpose of the  query  is  know  the  special  attributes
inherent within the Supreme Lord Krishna's divine transcendental nature  and
their qualities and activities.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
The Supreme Lord Krishna manifested His <i>visvarupa</i> or divine  universal  form
of wonderful and terrible visage  to  exhibit  His  unlimited  and  absolute
sovereign power. Now that Arjuna's request of seeing the <i>visvarupa</i> has  been
granted he beseeches further to know Lord Krishna's  intention  and  purpose
for exhibiting it and he desires to know who Lord  Krishna  truly  is  apart
from the <i>visvarupa.</i> He addresses Lord Krishna as <i>deva-vara</i> meaning  Lord  of
all lords and  is  curious  as  to  the  reason  why  He  exhibited  to  His
surrendered <i>bhakta</i> or devotee the terrible  visages  with  many  mouths  and
teeth along with His wonderful form of cosmic glory. Arjuna is now  desiring
to see Lord Krishna's pleasing and charming, sublime  humanlike  form.  Lord
Krishna exhibited  His  all  powerful  <i>visvarupa</i>    to  show  Arjuna  in  no
uncertain terms that without any effort He will destroy all  the  kings  and
warriors that are arrayed in the Kaurava army and this does  not  depend  at
all  upon  Arjuna  and  the  Pandava  army.  Lord  Krishna  is  prepared  to
annihilate the  complete  Kaurava  army  if  need  be  and  to  assert  this
conviction He exhibited the terrible visages of the <i>visvarupa</i> is  to  remind
Arjuna of this.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Arjuna had previously requested Lord Krishna to see His <i>visvarupa</i> or  divine
universal form. When Lord Krishna exhibited the  terrifying  aspect  of  the
<i>visvarupa</i> Arjuna became unbalanced  and  agitated  and  becoming  bewildered
stated <i>namo'stu te deva-vara</i> meaning I offer obeisance  to  you,  and  asked
the question to Lord Krishna: Who are you of such terrible form? Arjuna  saw
the terrible external  form  of  the  <i>visvarupa</i>  but  could  not  know  Lord
Krishna's intention for exhibiting it. Now he wishes to know  who  and  what
Lord Krishna is beyond the external manifestation of the <i>visvarupa.</i>
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 11, verse 31 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
