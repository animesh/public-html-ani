<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
In the two preceding verses  the  subservience  of  all  <i>jivas</i>  or  embodied
beings to <i>prakriti</i> or the material substratum pervading all  existence  have
been given according to the viewpoint of <i>sankhya</i>  or  analytical  reasoning.
Now Lord Krishna declares the conclusion in this verse and the next. As  has
been confirmed in many of the Vedic scriptures, within the etheric heart  of
all <i>jivas</i> abides  the  Supreme  Lord  as  <i>paramatma</i>  the  Supreme  Soul  who
monitors  the  thoughts  and  activities  of  all   living   entities.   The
Svetasvatara Upanisad VI.XI beginning <i>eko devah sarva  bhutesu</i>  states:  The
Supreme Lord who is one without a second abides in the core of the heart  in
all created beings as the  indwelling  monitor.  The  Briharanyaka  Upanisad
III.VII.XIII beginning <i>yah sarvesu  bhutesu  tisthan</i>  states:  The   Supreme
Soul abides within all beings yet He is  different  from  all  beings.  Whom
beings do not know.  Whose  creations  all  beings  are:  Who  controls  all
beings. Who is  the  inner  ruler  of  all  beings  as  well  as  the  inner
controller of all beings. How this is to be understood is inconceivable  but
by His illusory energy known as <i>maya</i> all beings  are  revolving  in  <i>samsara</i>
the perpetual cycle of birth and death engaged in a  myriad  of  activities.
The <i>jivas</i> who identify with the physical body are kept revolving life  after
life in material existence. The <i>jivas</i> who identify  with  the  Supreme  Lord
have the chance to transcend this by His grace.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><font size=2>There is no commentary for this verse.</font>
</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<i>Isvarah</i> is the all pervading Supreme Lord as the indwelling  monitor  within
<i>hrid dese</i> the core of the heart which is the source  of  all  consciousness,
the basis of all actions <i>pravritti</i> or passive  and  <i>nivritti</i>  or  energetic.
The Supreme Lord abides simultaneously in the etheric heart  of  every  <i>jiva</i>
or embodied being in a subatomic form known as <i>paramatma</i> the  Supreme  Soul.
Once so installed the Supreme Lord activates,  energises  and  animates  the
physical body of the <i>jiva</i> or embodied  being.  <i>Maya</i>  the  external  illusory
potency  bombasts  and  buffets  the  <i>jivas</i>  interminably  in  the  material
existence in accordance to and consistent with the <i>guna</i> or mode of  material
nature  which  is  predominate.  The   Brihadaranyaka   Upanisad   III.VI.XV
beginning <i>yah sarvesu bhutesu tisthan</i> states: The  Supreme  Lord  abides  in
all beings and controls all living entities  internally.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Before Lord Krishna spoke of doership as dependent upon material nature  and
the three <i>gunas</i> or modes of material nature that one is endowed  with.  This
was in line with the <i>samkhya</i> philosophy  of  analytical  reasoning.  Now  He
confirms that all <i>jivas</i> or embodied beings are under the  total  control  of
the Supreme Lord based upon the Vedic scriptures.  The  Taittiriya  Upanisad
III.IX.II beginning <i>sa yascayam purushe</i> states: The  Supreme  Lord  residing
on that plane ot  transcendent  eternity  is  the  same  Supreme  Lord  that
resides within the core of the  etheric  heart  of  all  <i>jivas</i>  o  embiodied
beings.  The  Brihadaranyaka  Upanisad  III.VIII.IX  beginning   <i>etasya   va</i>
<i>aksharasya</i> states: Verily by the Supreme Lords  command  the  sun  and  moon
maintain their fixed positions, the heavens and Earth keep  ther  respective
position, the seconds, minutes,  hours,  days,  nights,  weeks,  months  and
years all maintain their relative posions. The Supreme Lord is the  ultimate
controller of everything  animate and inanimate which are acting  under  His
external  energy  <i>prakriti</i>  the  material  substratum   pervading   physical
existence from whence arise three <i>gunas</i> or modes of  material  nature  which
determines all actions based upon the <i>karma</i> or reactions  to  previous  good
and evil actions enacted in  a  physical  body  and  five  senses  which  is
likened unto a machine.  The  purport  is  that  just  as  a  puppet  master
controls his puppets so does the Supreme Lord control the <i>jivas.</i>
<p>
