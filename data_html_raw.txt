<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
After bestowing the divine spark of spiritual intelligence the Supreme  Lord
Krishna residing within as <i>paramatma</i> the Supreme Soul makes sure it  remains
steady until His exclusive devotee eradicates  all  nescience  and  achieves
<i>atma tattva</i> or realisation of their eternal soul  and  subsequent  <i>moksa</i>  or
liberation from the material  existence  and  <i>samsara</i>  being  the  perpetual
cycle of birth and death. This is what  is  meant  by  the  words  <i>anukampa-</i>
<i>artham</i> which means He shows them special mercy out of great  compassion  for
their devotion, the Supreme Lord blesses them  and  by  His  Grace  all  the
darkness of material  existence  is  illuminated.  By  what  means  is  this
accomplished and how is this done? Lord Krishna anticipating  such  a  query
speaks the words  <i>atma-bhava-stho</i>  meaning  situated  within  their  etheric
heart as <i>paratmatma</i>, He illuminates their  consciousness  with  the  glowing
radiance of spiritual knowledge.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
It is due to the loving compassion felt by  the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna  for
those persons who dedicate and devote their lives to Him that He  is  always
manifest in their  hearts  and  minds  as  the  paramount  object  of  their
thoughts and plans and activities, accompanied by  the  realisation  of  His
transcendental qualities and attributes. The words <i>jnana-dipena</i> meaning  the
radiant light of knowledge is verily  Lord  Krishna  Himself  in  all  their
thoughts.  <i>Tamah</i>  or  the  darkness  of  ignorance  to  which   beings   are
continuously subjected to is due to past and present <i>karma</i> or the  reactions
to one's actions.  This  <i>tamah</i>  and  <i>karma</i>  are  extremely  antagonistic  to
spiritual intelligence and  produce  the  desire  for  things  material  and
temporary instead of  things  spiritual  and  eternal  which  lead  to  Lord
Krishna; but for His devotees He dispels their  <i>tamah</i>  and  dissolves  their
<i>karma.</i> Having comprehended the qualities, attributes and  character  of  the
Supreme Lord Krishna regarding His  <i>vibhuti</i>  or  phenomenal,  transcendental
potencies manifesting throughout  all  of  creation  and  His  <i>yoga</i>  or  the
science  of  the  individual  consciousness  attaining  communion  with  His
ultimate  consciousness,  eternally.  This  knowledge   will   mature   into
appreciation which will evolve into <i>bhakti</i> or exclusive loving devotion  for
Him and subsequently unlimited bliss will naturally arise within the  hearts
and minds of His devotees.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Nevertheless in order  to  shower  His  grace  exclusively  upon  those  who
worship Him in <i>bhakti</i>  or  exclusive  loving  devotion,  He  <i>atma-bhava-stho</i>
meaning manifests within their hearts <i>jnana-dipena</i> the radiant knowledge  of
enlightenment which completely dispels all <i>tamah</i> or  darkness  of  ignorance
due to unlimited <i>karma</i> or multitudinous reactions from  actions  since  time
immemorial.
<p>
