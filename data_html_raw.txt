<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The duties of both the <i>vaisyas</i> or  mercantile  agricultural  class  and  the
<i>sudras</i> or servant class is  explained  by  Lord  Krishna.  Cow  raising  and
protecting and agricultural cultivation  as  well  engaging  in  buying  and
selling of products and goods are duties of <i>vaisyas</i> born of  the  nature  of
<i>raja guna</i> or mode of passion mixed with <i>tama guna</i> or mode of ignorance.  The
duties of <i>sudras</i> is loyal service to the other three classes  and  receiving
sustenance for their livelihood from them and is born of the nature of  <i>tama</i>
<i>guna.</i>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><font size=2>There is no commentary for this verse.</font>
</td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The natural duties allocated to  the  <i>vaisyas</i>  or  agricultural,  mercantile
class are:
<i>1) krishi</i> or farming.
<i>2) go-raksa</i> or cow raising and cow protecting
<i>3) vanijyam</i>  or  acts  of  commerce  and  trade  endorsed  by  the  Vedic
scriptures.
The natural duties of <i>sudras</i> or worker class are  amenable  service  to  the
upper 3 classes.
<p>
Thus by defining the duties  of  the  four  classes  in  Vedic  culture  the
necessary performance of prescribed Vedic  activities  and  the  appropriate
participation of each of the four classes is implied. For example  in  <i>yagna</i>
or ritualistic performance of propitiation and  worship  the  <i>Brahmins</i>  will
perform the ceremony, the <i>ksatriya</i> will  donate  wealth,  the  <i>vaisyas</i>  will
supply the ingredients such as <i>ghee</i> or clarified butter  and  cow  products,
the <i>sudras</i> will serve the <i>ksatriyas</i> and <i>vaisyas.</i>
<p>
The Brahmins being naturally endowed with <i>sattva guna</i> the mode  of  goodness
are allotted with <i>samah</i> or self  control  of  the  mind  which  bestows  the
purity required to perform Vedic rituals and ceremonies. Although  qualified
for initiation this was not allocated to <i>ksatriya</i> and <i>vaisyas</i>  due  to  they
having a preponderance of <i>raja guna</i> mode of passion and <i>tama  guna</i>  mode  of
ignorance which is not compatible with <i>samah.</i> 
<p>
The Brahmins exemplify by example the  teachings  of  the  Vedic  scriptures
instructing others the proper way to live a  human  existence  according  to
the status of life they are   in.  The  <i>ksatriyas</i>  duty  is  to  righteously
govern and protect the citizens of the country.  The  <i>vaisyas</i>  duty  is  cow
protection and cultivating food for the population. The <i>sudras</i>  duty  is  to
do all the menial services for a fair compensation, necessary for the  upper
three classes to discharge their duties expediently and efficiently.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Now Lord Krishna reiterates the duties of the <i>vaisyas</i> the farmer  mercantile
class. Cow raising and cow protecting, tilling the land for  crops,  trading
and selling for business are the duities of <i>vaisyas.</i> Industrious service  to
the other three classes for fair  recompense  is  the  duty  of  <i>sudras</i>  the
worker class.
<p>
The Manu Samhita, chapter one,verses  88-91  confirm:  Study  of  the  Vedic
scriptures, teaching of the  Vedic  scriptures,  performing  Vedic  rituals,
officiating  as  priests  for  <i>ksatriyas</i>  and  <i>vaisyas</i>  initiated  in  Vedic
culture, giving gifts and accepting donations are the  duties  of  Brahmins.
Protection of the citizenry, supporting and organising Vedic rituals,  study
of the Vedic scriptures, righteousness in ruling  and  giving  donations  to
the Brahmin are the duties of <i>ksatriyas.</i> Protecting  and  raising  of  cows,
study of the Vedas, agriculture, gift making, buying and trading are  duties
of <i>vaisyas.</i> Only one service was ordained for <i>sudras</i> the  worker  class  and
that was to ungrudgingly serve the three upper social orders.
<p>
The qualities of controlling the mind and senses, physical restraint of  the
body,  etc.  are  commonly  prescribed  for  all  aspirants  of   <i>moksa</i>   or
liberation, they are naturally present in Brahmins due to  being  inherently
predominated by <i>sattva guna.</i> The Vedic scriptures cite examples that  others
due to <i>samskaras</i> or previous impressions may  also  be  situated  in  <i>sattva</i>
<i>guna</i>  as  well  if  possessing  truthfulness,   forgiveness,   non-violence,
humility, etc. and the various qualities of <i>sattva guna.</i> This is apparent.
<p>
