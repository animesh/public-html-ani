<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The cause of the variation in results is being stated by Lord  Krishna  that
from <i>sattva guna</i> or mode of  goodness  arises  liminousity  the  results  of
which are knowledge and happiness. From <i>raja guna</i> or  the  mode  of  passion
arises desires the results which are greed and suffering. From <i>tama guna</i>  or
the mode of ignorance comes illusion in the form of madness and delusion.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
What Lord Krishna is stating about those situated  in  <i>sattva  guna</i>  or  the
mode of goodness increase their wisdom  to  fruition  then  <i>atma  tattva</i>  or
realisation of the immortal soul becomes a reality. When <i>raja  guna</i>  or  the
mode of passion increases in intensity more  and  more  avid  appetites  are
incited to enjoy the fruits of. Similarly when <i>tama  guna</i>  or  the  mode  of
ignorance  increases  it  manifests  as  perverted  understanding  and   the
proclivity to be influenced by wickedness from which more and more  <i>tama</i>  is
begotten. Thus all developement of intelligence is blocked and neutralised.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna is explaining that concerning the <i>gunas</i> or the three  modes  of
material nature the variations  in  the  results  is  attributed  originally
within the respective charcterisitics of  each  quality.  The  results  from
<i>sattva guna</i> or mode of goodness is knowledge which  has  understanding  that
is illuminated and from that gives great happiness. The  results  from  <i>raja</i>
<i>guna</i> or mode of passion is unabated desires which  incites  incessant  greed
for wealth and constant hankering for  sense  gratificationfrom  form  which
comes pain by the endeavor and suffering from the  loss.  The  results  from
<i>tama guna</i> or the mode of ignorance is  nescience  and  inertia  which  gives
delusion and forces one to be in the darkness of illusion.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 14, verse 17 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
