<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Exactly how Lord Krishna is the cause  of  the  sustenance  of  creation  is
being described in five verses commencing here. His being  the  sapidity  or
essence in water means He exists in the molecular  substratum  of  water  as
the essence of sapidity. Likewise He is the luster  in  the  sun  and  moon,
meaning He exists in them as there wonderful  illuminating  substratum.  The
other examples that follow are also to be explained in a similar manner.  In
all the Vedas representing enlightened and articulate sound, He is the  root
syllable <i>OM</i> which is His personal transcendental sound vibration<i>.</i> He is  the
essence of all sounds in ether and He is the potency of competence  in  man.
Verily man excels and flourishes by the potency of competence.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Here we are receiving <i>jnana</i> or knowledge that Lord Krishna  is  the  essence
of specific things. Super sensory illuminating perception  is  <i>vijnana.</i>  The
greatness of the Supreme Lord is revealed later but now  even  the  inherent
nature of and His being the essence of along with the natural qualities  and
attributes of different substances are  being  described  as  being  special
potencies and representations of the  Supreme  Lord.  The  fact  that  these
special essences and attributes do not manifest on their  own  is  shown  by
the mentioning of special words such as <i>raso'ham  apsu</i>  meaning  He  is  the
essence in water. This essence is to be understood in the  case  of  <i>upasana</i>
or spiritual practices. It has been mentioned in the Gita  Kalpa  that:  The
attribute of essence  in  various  flavours  and  its  inherent  nature  are
basically  due  to  the   perennial   principle   of   <i>dharma</i>   or   eternal
righteousness. The provider and enjoyer of the essence is always solely  the
Supreme Lord Krishna, who as the essence resides  in  all  living  entities.
All the empowered demigods  are  mere  reflections  of  His  splendour.  The
Supreme Lord is the essence of all and should be earnestly sought  by  those
who seek the essence of life. The inherent energy of  <i>Prakriti</i>  or  material
nature and all <i>jivas</i> or embodied beings is all ordained by the Supreme  Lord
and all natural propensities and inherent tendencies are bequeathed  by  His
grace alone. There is nothing whatsoever  animate  or  inanimate  throughout
all of creation that does not contain the Supreme Lords essence.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The Gita Kalpa has  stated  that:  Desires  not  contrary  to  righteousness
should be engaged in  by  an  aspirant  of  <i>moksa</i>  or  liberation  from  the
material  existence.  For  the  purpose   of   elevating   one's   spiritual
development one should only perform activities which are  not  contradictory
to <i>dharma</i> but even  the  renunciation  of  such  authorised  activities  are
recommended to be relinquished for advanced aspirants. Strength without  the
debilitating influence of desires and attachments should be  achieved.  Then
by the grace of the  Supreme  Lord  perfection  will  be  attained  and  the
desired goal will be reached.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Residing in water as sapidity, the Supreme Lord is  known  as  the  essence.
Residing in the sun and the moon as luster, He  is  known  as  illumination.
Residing in the Vedas as His very self, He is known as the <i>pranava</i>  original
sound vibration <i>OM.</i> Residing in the ether as sound Lord Krishna is known  as
the word.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Now to illustrate that He is the form of all because He is the <i>atma</i> or  soul
of all, Lord Krishna reveals in the next five verses how He is  situated  as
the core essence of all properties beginning with that He is the essence  of
water in its subtle casual form as sapidity. He is the essence  of  the  sun
and moon being illumination. He is  the  supremely  sacred  sound  vibration
<i>OM.</i> He is the  origin  of  all  the  Vedas  and  the  source  of  the  Vedic
utterances which become objects of hearing. He is the subtle casual form  in
ether and the potency of competence in men.
<p>
