<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Now  Lord  Krishna  gives  the  defects  in  not  believing  and   following
prescribed Vedic activities. Those who are  inimical  to  the  teachings  of
Bhagavad-Gita and are antagonistic to the injunction of the Vedas  that  all
activities should be performed in <i>yagna</i> or worship for the  satisfaction  of
the Supreme Lord. Such people should  be  known  as  fools  and  lacking  in
discrimination and all of their activities contrary to this  are  doomed  to
failure.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
In these verses Lord Krishna is speaking about the merits one receives  from
following the teachings of the Bhagavad-Gita. Those who  perform  prescribed
Vedic activities with renunciation gradually achieve <i>moksa</i> or liberation  by
<i>jnana yoga</i> the path of cultivating Vedic knowledge. Who then  can  calculate
the position of one who has attained <i>atma-tattva</i> or  soul  realisation.  Now
<i>karma yoga</i> the path of performing prescribed Vedic actions is not  given  as
another medium of attainment. Renunciation and dedication of  one's  actions
are  solely  for  the  sake  of  realising  the  Supreme  Being  only.   For
realisation of the Supreme  Being,  liberation  does  not  depend  upon  one
particular path. In the Narayana Shataksara it is  stated  that:  All  paths
may or may not award <i>moksa</i> to the  dedicated  aspirant  treading  upon  them
depending upon their level of renunciation in the performance of  unattached
actions.  But renunciation by itself,  not  being  dependent  upon  anything
else is capable of awarding <i>moksa</i> to those who fully perform  it<i>.</i>  Therefore
the contention that <i>jnana yoga</i> and <i>karma yoga</i> alone are sufficient to  award
<i>moksa</i> is not correct.
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
Lord Krishna is revealing the eternal truth that He  is  the  <i>atma</i>  or  soul
within all beings in existence, be they human, animal, aquatic, plant, demi-
gods or  any  other  species  of  life  that  was  not  cloned.  He  is  the
maintainer, sustainer and monitor of all beings through the  medium  of  the
<i>atma</i> and all of its impulses come from  Him.  All  souls  emanate  from  the
Supreme Lord and because of Him are completely spiritual  and  eternal.  All
the souls of all the living beings in creation everywhere in creation  as  a
total  aggregate  comprise  the  form  of  the   Supreme   Lord's   eternal,
transcendental  spiritual  body.  Whoever  does  not  avail  themselves  and
conformably act to this eternal truth, or take this eternal truth  seriously
in earnest, or who deems unnecessary and not worth the effort,  this  person
should be understood to be completely destitute of knowledge and  unable  to
evolve spiritually and are  incapable of achieving <i>atma tattva</i> or  knowledge
of the soul become lost.  Spiritual  intelligence  is  what  determines  the
light of knowledge in the material existence. In the  absence  of  spiritual
knowledge, darkness and ignorance prevails and knowledge  becomes  erroneous
and defective. Thus it has been illustrated that doership transpires due  to
the union of <i>prakriti</i>  or  material  nature  and  the  physical  body  being
influenced by the <i>gunas</i> or modes of  goodness,  passion  and  nescience  and
this is dependent ultimately upon the Supreme Lord.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Reflecting in this manner it can be ascertained that <i>karma yoga</i> or the  path
of performing prescribed Vedic actions is  appropriate  for  one  performing
<i>jnana yoga</i> or the path of cultivating Vedic knowledge as  well.  <i>Karma  yoga</i>
is appropriate on account of it being  easy  to  perform,  exempt  from  the
danger of failure and requires no  assistance  from  other  methods  in  its
application. Contrarily <i>jnana yoga</i> is difficult to practice, susceptible  to
failure and must perform some <i>karma yoga</i> anyway in order to maintain  health
and vigor which is essential for body maintenance if one is to  perform  any
yoga at all. Also it has been pointed out how it is necessary for  a  person
of distinction situated in Vedic wisdom to set an  example  to  inspire  the
common man for the benefit and welfare of the world.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
What difficulties and dangers are fraught for the performers of  <i>jnana  yoga</i>
are now explained in the remainder of this chapter.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Here Lord Krishna points out that those persons who disrespect
and ignore and refuse to follow the edicts and injunctions  of
the Bhagavad-Gita that are for the benefit of all mankind  are
fools, bereft of spiritual knowledge and in such  a  state  of
ignorance  all  their  activities  are  fruitless   and   they
hopelessly revolve in the cycle of birth and death, birth  and
death, birth and death, ad infinitum.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 3, verse 32 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
