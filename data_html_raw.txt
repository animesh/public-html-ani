<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The query may arise that  if  when  attaining  the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna's
eternal abode the <i>jiva</i>  or  embodied  being  does  not  return  to  material
existence then at the time of <i>pralaya</i> or  dissolution  when  all  <i>jivas</i>  are
merged into  His  manifestation  of  the  <i>brahman</i>  or  spiritual  substratum
pervading all  existence  they  become  one  essence  as  confirmed  in  the
Chandogya Upanisad VI.IX.II beginning  <i>te  yatha  tatra  na  vivekamlabhante</i>
meaning: As the pollen of different  flowers forms the essence of honey  and
lose their individual identities in the same manner  all  <i>jivas</i>  lose  their
individual existences when they merge into the <i>brahman</i>  although  they  know
not in their varigated forms from whence they have come forth.  If  this  is
the case then who really is  a  transmigrating  <i>jiva.</i>  Apprehending  such  a
query Lord Krishna describes a  <i>samsarin</i>  or  transmigrating  <i>jiva</i>  in  this
verse and the next four. Lord Krishna explains that an  eternal  portion  of
Himself is within the etheric heart of every <i>jiva</i> in existence in  the  form
of <i>paramatma</i> the eternal, omnipresent supreme soul which exists next to  the
<i>atma</i> or individual immortal soul as a witness. The <i>jiva</i> although eternal  in
essence having an eternal portion of the  Supreme  Lord,  through  ignorance
and false identification of the physical body becomes a  <i>samsarin</i>  drawn  by
the desires and attachments of the previous existence to suffer or enjoy  as
precisely dictated by <i>karma</i> or the reactions to actions  performed  in  past
lives. This is accomplished on the subtle plane  by  a  specifc  and  unique
algorithm for every <i>jiva</i> along with senses,  mind  and  vital  force   which
involuntarily draws them back down into mundane existence  on  the  physical
plane automatically  adjusting  the  <i>karma</i>  and  tabulating  the  merits  or
demerits from the exact same position by which they were merged at the  time
of <i>pralaya.</i> So although it is true that all <i>jivas</i> attain the  state  of  the
Supreme Lords <i>brahman</i> or spiritual substratum pervading  all  existence.  At
the time of dissolution they are unconscious of it and of the  Supreme  Lord
due to being covered in ignorance, over burdened  with  the  impressions  of
past lives and overwhelmed by the <i>karma</i> of  the  previous  life.  Thus  with
such consciousness they only  qualify  to  attain  His  transitory  external
nature of <i>prakriti</i> or the material substratum pervading  physical  existence
and not His eternal transcendental nature as <i>brahman.</i> Therefore  such  <i>jivas</i>
revolve in <i>samsara</i> the perpetual cycle of birth and death due  to  ignorance
of their eternal nature and attachment to the  desires  and  sense  objects.
But for one situated in <i>atma tattva</i> or realisation of the immortal  soul  by
<i>bhakti</i> or exclusive loving devotion unto the Supreme  Lord  there  is  never
mandatory return to material existence.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Lord Krishna explains a manifestation of His divine form. Due  to  the  <i>atma</i>
or immortal soul although distinctly different is similar in quality to  the
Supreme Lord it is sometimes spoken  of  as  a  partial  manifestation.  The
Supreme Lord when enveloping the physical body, energises  the  five  senses
and  activates  the  mind  relative  to  <i>prakriti</i>  the  material  substratum
pevading physical existence. The statement that the  mind  is  drawn  toards
sound may give rise to the misnomer that  the  <i>jiva</i>  or  embodied  being  is
independent. Lord Krishna clarifies this in the very next verse..
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The <i>atma</i> or immortal soul within all <i>jivas</i> or  embodied  beings  constitutes
an eternal portion of Lord Krishna and thus is  also  eternal.  Yet  because
the <i>jiva</i> is inextricably emeshed in material existence from time  immemorial
it is enslaved by the nescience of its own <i>karma</i>  or  reactions  to  actions
and revolves incessantly in  <i>samsara</i>  the  perpetrual  cycle  of  birth  and
death. Precisely based on this <i>karma</i> a <i>jiva</i> is forced to accept  a  suitable
body such as a demigod, human, animal etc., attracting with it  five  senses
and a mind exactly appropriate to maximising the  chances  of  survival  for
such a  <i>jiva.</i>  But  when  the  <i>jiva</i>  through  association  of  the  Vaisnava
spiritual  master  becomes  enlightened   from   hearing   his   unequivocal
instructions and surrenders fully unto the Supreme  Lord  Krishna  as  one's
only refuge then Lord Krishna Himself releases the <i>jiva</i> from the bondage  of
<i>samsara</i> and the <i>jiva</i> becomes situated in <i>atma-tattva</i>  or  soul  realisation.
Other  than  taking  exclusive  shelter  of  Lord  Krishna  or  any  of  His
authorised incarnations as revealed in Vedic sciptures it is impossible  for
the <i>jiva</i> which is  very  much  handicapped  in  terms  of  intelligence  and
potency to achieve <i>moksa</i> or liberation. This is due to the heavy  burden  of
unresolved <i>karma</i> attached to the jiva that they must carry  with  them  life
after life. But all actions performed for the satisfaction  of  the  Supreme
Lord have no <i>karma</i> attached to them. This is the difference.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
It might be asked that since the attributes and potencies  manifesting  from
the Supreme Lord Krishna are His eternal portions; then why  does  the  <i>atma</i>
or immortal soul which also manifests from Him and  is  an  eternal  portion
subject  to  the  forced  incarceration  of  transmigration.  Lord   Krishna
clarifies this point by stating that finite reflections cannot be proved  of
having eternality neither that they are  beginningless  .  This  is  because
they are generated due to accessories and adjuncts such as water  reflecting
the sunlight. The sun is always present but it may  be  obscured  by  clouds
and then again there is no reflection at all during night. Because  the  sun
is perceived externally by reflection it is known to exist  but  to  peceive
the <i>atma</i> in its eternal state it must be perceived internally. In  this  way
due to limitations of the <i>jiva</i> or embodied being, there are some  conditions
which cannot be seen, experienced or perceived  attached  to  realising  the
<i>atmas</i> eternality which is beyond the purview of the mind  and  senses.  Thus
the <i>atma</i> cannot be proved by reflection. One in nescience and  ignorance  by
the obscuration of knowledge is  unable  to  ever  understand  the  <i>atma</i>  or
comprehend its infinite nature; so  how  can  one  imagine  its  reflection.
Contrarily if the finite nature of the  <i>atma</i>  is  accepted  the  <i>atma</i>  still
cannot be proved  as  a  reflection  because  of  the  established  doctrine
confirmed in the Mundaka Upanisad III.I.IX beginning:  <i>eso  anuratma  cetasi</i>
<i>veditavyo</i> meaning: The <i>atma</i> or immortal soul is infinitesimal and  difficult
to  realise  but  when  one  achieves  spiritual  enlightenment   by   self-
realisation the <i>atma</i> shines within  the  etheric  heart.  Also  the  Vedanta
Sutras II.III.XVIII beginning <i>utcranti gat yagatinam</i>  states:  The  <i>atma</i>  is
infintesimal, sub-atomic and able to pass in and  out  from  body  to  body.
Also in II.III.XXII beginning <i>guna dva lokavat</i> meaning: The <i>atma</i> resides  in
the heart but by its potency of consciousness pervades  all  the  body  like
candle light pervading a room. So although the individual  soul  is  eternal
it is infintesimal; but <i>paramatma</i> the Supreme  soul  is  eternal,  unlimited
and infinite.
<p>
The conclusion is that if an adjunct is infinite it  can  not  be  reflected
because it is beyond perception. Thus any conception of  the  <i>atma</i>  being  a
reflecion of the Supreme Lord is illusion and would be like considering  the
suns rays to be a reflection of the sun.  Such  ideas  and  conceptions  are
false and  delusory  and  contradict  Vedic  scriptures.  So  whenever  Lord
Krishna speaks of His portion or His potency, it always  denotes  eternality
as  every  part  of  Him  is  eternal  and  is  not  a  seperate  entity  or
consciousness. Lord Krishna already declared in chapter 7, verse 5  that  he
has an internal and external nature. Although the  power  and  the  powerful
appear as seperate energies they cannot  exist  independent  of  each  other
even though eternally exemplifying unity in diversity  as  is  evidenced  by
the <i>jiva</i> or embodied being, which is confined to inhaling air  or  water  as
the case may be in various organisms. The <i>jiva</i> has been  bewildered  by  the
latent  tendncy  of  enjoying  sense  objects  since  time  immemorial   and
subsequently ensnared by their actions to the law of <i>karma</i> or  reactions  to
actions from such material enjoyment and exploitation  by  the  five  senses
which awards every <i>jiva</i> the appropriate body  to  inhabit  corresponding  to
what one eats and what reactions one has acquired in te form of  the  effect
called the indivual flase ego.
<p>
Those not fully situated in spiritual knowledge have the  opinion  that  the
<i>atma</i> is the highest self and  that  it  only  appears  limited  due  to  the
covering of nescience which is like ether  appearing limited in  a  pitcher.
When the limiting adjuncts are no longer in contact  with  each  other  then
the ether is perceived without limitations and in  the  same  way  when  the
adjunct of nescience is removed the true nature of  the  <i>atma</i>  is  revealed.
But this supposition is directly refuted in this verse by the word  <i>sanatana</i>
meaning eternal because it is a part of Lord Krishna. The singular usage  of
the compound words <i>jiva-bhuta</i> refers to the category of  <i>jiva</i>  and  includes
all <i>jivas</i> in every dimension throughout all creation.
<p>
