<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
Here <i>prakriti</i> or the material substratum pervading physical existence  which
has 24 categories consisting of the mind, ego, five  organs  of  perception,
the five senses, etc.is specifically meant by the word <i>ksetra</i> or  the  field
of activity. Yet since the non-distinctiveness of matter as I  and  mine  is
evolved as such only in the physical body, in order  to  differentiate  from
it this body is called  the  <i>ksetra.</i>  To  clarify  in  further  detail  Lord
Krishna proposes to explain what the <i>ksetra</i> referred to by Him is in  itself
such as inert, of the nature of its object, etc. What is  it  like  such  as
possessed  of  qualities  such  as  desire  and  aversion.  What   are   its
modifications such as which senses is it endowed with.  How  does  it  arise
meaning how is it in conjunction with <i>prakriti</i> and the <i>brahman.</i> What is  its
form and how is it directed to be either sentient or insentient in just  one
of innumerably unlimited varieties of species. Also what  is  the  <i>ksetrajna</i>
or knower of the <i>ksetra</i> in reality and what power does it have by virtue  of
its transcendental and inconceivable majesty by the potency  it  is  endowed
with. All these things Lord Krishna will now explain in brief.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
What the <i>yad viktri</i> of the <i>ksetra</i> means what are its transformations and  by
what modifications is it affected. <i>Yatas ca yat</i> means  by  Him  the  Supreme
Lord the <i>ksetra</i> has  originated and that it is He alone  who  energized  it.
The words <i>sa ca ya</i> specifies the Supreme Lord also and indicates His form.
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
The words <i>yatas ca yat</i> indicates that solely by the energy  of  the  Supreme
Lord all creation is energized. He is  the  sole  energizer.  <i>Prerana</i>  means
having similarity in thoughts. Sometimes  even  dialogue  is  called  <i>samvad</i>
similar talk. Since Lord Krishna energizes all creation  He  also  energizes
the thoughts of all and sometimes such thoughts manifest which  are  similar
to His own. This is natural. So He is known as <i>anusarini</i>  or  <i>anusmanta</i>  the
ordainer of thoughts which have divine origins in Him. The <i>ksetrajna</i> is  the
<i>atma</i> or eternal soul and is <i>upadrishta</i> or  the  witness  with  supra-natural
connecting abilities for thoughts and things divine which emanate  from  the
Supreme Lord and induces this in those  who  seek  self-realisation  of  the
<i>atma</i> and attainment of the ultimate consciousness. Lord Krishna infers  that
this can be known is by its effect.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Now Lord Krishna  is  more  explicitly  explaining  details  about  the  <i>tat</i>
<i>ksetram</i> or field of activity.  Its  <i>yadrik  ca</i>  or  its  substantial  nature
regarding its use and function and its  purpose.  Also  its  <i>yad  vikari</i>  or
transformations and modifications along with its <i>yatas</i>  or  from  whence  it
came meaning its origin and how it  was  created  and  for  whose  use.  Yat
refers to its attributes and qualities. Also He is inferring that  knowledge
about the intrinsic nature of the <i>ksetra-jna</i>  as the knower  of  the  <i>ksetra</i>
which is the <i>atma</i> or eternal soil will be revealed as well  along  with  its
inherent potencies which is confirmed  by  the  word  <i>prabhavas  ca</i>  meaning
virtues, potency and powers. Lord Krishna will give a summary of  all  these
things.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Now Lord Krishna explains in brief the true nature of the <i>ksetra</i>  the  field
of activity and the <i>ksetra-jna</i> the knower of  the  field.  The  relationship
the  <i>ksetra</i>  has  with  the  <i>ksetra-jna</i>  and  the  consciousness  which   is
established in Him. Knowledge of  the  <i>ksetra</i>  means  what  type  entity  is
embodied and the qualities are what modifications restricts it and  what  is
its means of evolving itself  through the interrelationship  of  matter  and
spirit. And of the <i>ksetra-jna</i> exactly what is the <i>atma</i> or eternal soul,  who
is the knower of the <i>ksetra</i> and what is its powers and potencies.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 13, verse 4 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
