<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
Others refers to those in <i>jnana yoga</i> or cultivation of Vedic  knowledge  who
meditate in self-purification by offering all their senses like hearing  and
seeing along with all their organs like the ears and eyes and offer as  well
the assimilation of all food and drink along with the  ten  vital  airs  and
even offering  the inhaling breath into  the  exhaling  breath.  <i>Krikara</i>  is
known as producing hunger, <i>naga</i> is to  be  known  as  eructation,  <i>kurma</i>  as
opening the eyelids, <i>devadatta</i> is the function  of  yawning  and  <i>dhananjaya</i>
permeates the entire physical body remining even  after  death.  How  is  it
that even these functions are dissolved? They are evaporated by the fire  of
self-control and  <i>yoga</i>  or  the  science  of  the  individual  consciousness
communing with the ultimate consciousness. Meditation by focusing  the  mind
on the <i>atma</i> or soul is <i>yoga.</i> This <i>yoga</i> is the fire  that  which  is  kindled
and lit by  the  knowledge  of  the  <i>Brahman</i>  or  the  spiritual  substratum
pervading all existence. Lord Krishna purport is that by  knowledge  of  the
goal of meditation and concentrating the mind on the  <i>atma</i>  to  realise  it,
all the functions of the body naturally induced to cease.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The compound words atma-samyama-yogagnau means with the intelligence
controlled by the soul by the fire of yoga or the individual consciousness
in communion with the ultimate consciousness. This infers that due to
spiritual wisdom and knowledge an intensity of self-restraint and
determination is activated. It must be emphasised here that mere mechanical
control of the senses will not bring the desired result. When the mind has
achieved full knowledge and wisdom it perceives that it is no longer needs
to control the senses because by discrimination the senses are naturally
restrained and one becomes qualified to gain communion with the ultimate
consciousness.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
Others offer all the functions of the senses and all the  functions  of  the
<i>prana</i> or life breath along with the vital energy of the body in the fire  of
the purified soul ignited by knowledge. By  this  Lord  Krishna  means  that
such persons direct their efforts in disciplining the mind to  refrain  from
its pursuit of sensual activities.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna speaks of  more  qualified  <i>yogis</i>  or  one  who  practices  the
science  of  the  individual  consciousness  attaining  communion  with  the
ultimate consciousness. These <i>yogis</i> are free  from  attachment  to  external
sense objects because their senses are already under  complete  control.  In
this way they offer all their senses such as seeing and hearing and all  the
natural functions of the body such as speaking, eating,  drinking  and  even
breathing as they offer the inhalation into the exhalation and  vice  versa.
Eructation of the body such as the belching  sound  heard  from  eating  and
drinking is to keep the body balanced by letting out air. All  these  things
these <i>yogis</i> offer in the fire  of  self-purification  because  by  doing  so
all negative effects are consumed. This allows the  mind  to  be  controlled
and not diverted to extraneous things making  it  qualified  for  meditation
kindled by wisdom giving firm resolve and  fixed  determination  to  achieve
the goal of <i>atma tattva</i> or soul realisation.
<p>
