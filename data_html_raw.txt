<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The three fold nature of <i>sraddha</i> or faith can be determined by  a  <i>jivas</i>  or
embodied beings mode of worship. Those situated in <i>sattva guna</i> the  mode  of
goodness worship the Supreme Lord Krishna and His  authorised  incarnations.
Those situated in <i>raja guna</i> the mode  of  passion  pay  homage  to  demoniac
entities for power, wealth, fame and pleasure. Those situated in  <i>tama  guna</i>
the mode of ignorance pray to abstract gods with vague qualities as well  as
to ghosts and spirits. Thus by their actions their position and  quality  of
faith can be clearly discerned.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Those who are imbued with <i>sattva guna</i> the mode of goodness become  attracted
to activities in purity and goodness and they  are  devoted  to  worshipping
the Supreme Lord with full faith, concerned  only  with  the  transcendental
bliss derived from <i>bhakti</i> or exclusive  loving  devotion  unto  the  Supreme
Lord Krishna which is completely transcendental to  material  existence  and
untainted by darkness and suffering. Those in <i>raja guna</i> or mode  of  passion
offer diabolical rituals to demoniac entities from the physical  and  astral
realms to gain pleasure, power, wealth and fame.  The  results  are   bitter
sweet when succeeding and bitterly painful when failing because one  has  to
give something physical in return such as the death of a  family  member  or
one's health. Those in <i>tama guna</i> or mode of  ignorance  pay  homage  to  the
ghosts and spirits which give no joy and is filled with  pain  and  anguish.
The results of these three <i>gunas</i> or modes of material nature are  determined
directly by whichever one is situated in  along  with  the  quality  of  the
faith in which the worshipper is performing even  if  it  is  in  accordance
with the Vedic scriptures. Lord Krishna  has  categorically  emphasised  and
declared that not even the most miniscule modicum of  happiness  is  derived
from any activity which is contrary and opposed  to  the  Vedic  scriptures;
which embody all that is for  the  highest  good  for  all.  Those  who  are
inimical to the Vedic scriptures which benefit all  creation  are  evil  and
possess a demoniac nature.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna is stating that  the  position  and  status  of  all  <i>jivas</i>  or
embodied beings and their category can be determined by understanding  their
<i>sraddha</i>  or  faith.  The  three  kinds  of  <i>sraddha</i>  can  be  observed   and
specifically categorised from indicatory signs in the form of their mode  of
worship, whom they worship, whether or  not  the  worship  is   abstract  or
finites, etc. Those endowed with a preponderance of <i>sattva guna</i> or  mode  of
goodness worship  the  Supreme  Lord  and  their  faith  is  saturated  with
goodness. Those possessed with a preponderance  of  <i>raja  guna</i>  or  mode  of
passion pay homage to demoniac entities for power  and  dominion  and  their
faith is saturated with passion. Those possessed  with  a  preponderance  of
<i>tama guna</i> or the mode of ignorance pray to  ghosts  and  spirits  and  their
faith is saturated with ignorance.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 17, verse 4 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
