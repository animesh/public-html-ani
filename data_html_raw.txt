<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Commencing with this  verse  until  the  conclusion  of  this  chapter  Lord
Krishna reveals His prominent <i>vibhuti</i>  or  divine,  transcendental  opulence
beginning with of the 12 Adityas He manifests Himself as  Visnu  incarnation
manifesting in the form of the brahmin dwarf Vamana.  Of  luminaries  He  is
the solar orbs, the radiant suns whose shining rays illuminate the  darkness
of unlimited, innumerable universes.  Of the  <i>Maruts</i>  the  seven  groups  of
winds which flow throughout all space atmosphere, Lord Krishna is  the  wind
known as Parivaha which precedes all the others and bears the  name  <i>Marici.</i>
It should not be misconstrued that Lord Krishna is talking about one of  the
six great sages who were mind born by Brahma also  with  the  same  name  as
that is not correct. The words <i>naksatranam aham sasi</i> means that as the  moon
He is Lord over the 27 constellations  beginning  with  Ashvini  and  ending
with Revati due to the moon having a stronger influence. In this  verse  the
word <i>Vishnur</i> is in the partitive case whereas in others the case  ending  is
possessive. Although form this verse onwards the meanings  are  quite  clear
we shall show at selective verses throughout this  chapter  that  even  with
regard to incarnations of the Supreme  Lord  Krishna  such  as  Vishnu,  the
intention  of  describing  them  shows  that  their  superlative  power   is
exemplified as also a part of His <i>vibuti.</i>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify>The Supreme Lord Krishna  is  called  Vishnu  who  is  one  of  His  primary
incarnations because He pervades all spaces. <i>Vishva</i> means all pervading  and
<i>vish</i> means entering into. The Moksa Dharma states: That the Supreme Lord  is
the goal for all creatures and conscious beings. By Him  is  the  world  and
the sky enveloped and His  glory  is  unlimited  and  immeasurable.  Abiding
within all living entities He desires their preservation and  best  welfare.
Because of His strident all encompassing steps  He  is  known  as  Trivikram
another name for Vamanadeva an incarnation of Vishnu.  It  should  be  noted
that His <i>vibhuti</i>  or  divine,  transcendental  opulence  of  <i>Marici</i>  is  not
referring to one of the ancient sages of antiquity. His  <i>vibhuti</i>  of  <i>Marici</i>
is the primal breath that sweeps across  all  space   preceding  the  <i>Maruts</i>
which are the different types of powerful  winds  throughout  the  unlimited
trillions of universes.  The Rig Veda I.XXII.XVIII beginning <i>trini  pada  vi</i>
<i>chakrame vishnur gopa adabhyah ato dharmani dharayan</i> states: In three  great
strides Vishnu the protecter, who  is  undefeatable  upholds  the  perennial
principles of <i>sanatan dharma</i> or eternal righteousness.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The 12 Adityas are revealed in  the  Vishnu  Purana  being  Dhatri,  Aryama,
Mitra, Varuna, Indra, Vivasvan, Pushan, Parjanya, Amsu, Bhaga,  Tvastri  and
Vishnu.  The  Supreme  Lord  Krishna's  <i>vibhuti</i>  or  divine   transcendental
opulence is the one bearing the name Vishnu who is chief  of  them  all.  Of
the lights that shine in the countless universes Lord Krishna's  <i>vibhuti</i>  is
the solar group consisting of  all  suns.  Of  the  7  Maruts  being  Avaha,
Prahava, Nivaha, Purvaha, Udvaha, Samvaha and  Parivaha  who  are  different
varieties of winds blowing throughout space, Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i>  is  the
chief wind known as Parivaha who bears  the  name  <i>Marici</i>  the  primal  wind
which precedes all the others. Of all constellations Lord Krishna's  <i>vibhuti</i>
is the moon. The genitive  case  of  the  word  <i>sasi</i>  meaning  moon  is  not
referring to  one  out  of  many  but  is  specifying  one  over  all.  Thus
<i>naksatranam aham sasi</i> refers to the moon as being  the  Lord  over  all  the
constellations. This will also be exemplified similarly in  the  next  verse
where He states His <i>vibhuti</i> is the consciousness in all beings  which  means
that what exists as consciousness  apart from intelligence  is  His  <i>vibhuti</i>
also.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
From now until the end of the chapter the Supreme  Lord  Krishna  enumerates
His <i>vibhuti</i> or divine, transcendental opulence. He reveals He is  Vishnu  of
the 12 Adityas, All solar orbs whose  rays  illuminates  unlimited  galaxies
and universes. He is  the  Parivaha  wind  which  precedes  all  the  others
throughout  space  bearing  the  name  <i>Marici</i>  and  among  <i>naksatranams</i>   or
different constellations exercising their sphere of influence  Lord  Krishna
is the moon which is superior to all of them in influence.
<p>
