<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Why is such an enlightened person said to see  <i>saman</i>  or  equally?  This  is
being explained by Lord Krishna. By  seeing  the  resplendent  Supreme  Lord
everywhere, within oneself, within all beings and  within  all  things;  one
does not perform any activity that will degrade the <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul
of themselves  nor  degrade  the  <i>atma</i>  of  others.  This  means  they  have
recognised the Supreme Being within all beings and  does  not  deny  through
capriciousness or  ignorance  the  presence  of  <i>paramatma</i>  or  the  Supreme
immortal soul whose  very  nature  is  <i>sat</i>  or  eternal  existence,  <i>cit</i>  or
unlimited consciouness and <i>ananda</i>  never ending bliss. Such  an  enlightened
being is qualified to achieve <i>moksa</i> or liberation  from  material  existence
and attain the eternal spiritual worlds which are  <i>param  gati</i>  the  supreme
destination.  But  one  who  is  unable  to  see  equally  in  this   manner
erroneously looks upon their physical body as being the  same  as  the  <i>atma</i>
thinking that when the physical body perishes the <i>atma</i> perishes with it  and
thus mistakenly sentence the to <i>atma</i> to be non-existent. The  Isa  Upanisad,
verse III beginning <i>asurya nama te loka andhena</i> meaning:  The  demoniac  and
the demons degrade the <i>atma,</i> totally ignorant of its eternal  divine  nature
and are forced to enter the dark, gloomy hellish worlds in their next  life.
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Lord Krishna is  explaining  that  one  who  perceives  and  recognises  the
Supreme Being in every being is one who actually sees reality. The  <i>jiva</i>  or
embodied being practically  witnesses  death  and  destruction  in  material
existence everyday along  with  its  associated  misery.  The  Padma  Purana
states that as a form of misery the <i>jiva</i> sees itself; yet devoid  of  misery
is the Supreme Lord residing within  every  <i>jiva</i>  equally  from  Brahma  the
highest material being down  to  an  ant.  The  Supreme  Lord  expansion  as
<i>paramatma</i> or the Supreme Soul within all <i>jivas</i> throughout all dimensions  of
creation  does  not  diminish  or  degrade   His   transcendental   position
whatsoever, whether abiding in a demigod, human, animal etc. Perceiving  the
reality of the Supreme Lord in this way one is not subject to illusion.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
The Supreme Lord Krishna is  known  as  <i>isvara</i>  is  residing  <i>saman</i>  equally
<i>sarvatra</i> within all beings. This includes demigods,  humans,  animals,  etc.
The word <i>pasyati</i> here meaning perceives denotes that the  <i>atma</i>  or  immortal
soul is recognised wherever it may abide. By thus comprehending  the  actual
nature of the <i>atma</i> one  never  degrades  themselves  and  <i>na  hinasti</i>  never
causes harm to oneself. Contrarily if one insists on  identifying  the  <i>atma</i>
in the same way as  the  physical  body  in  the  differentiating  light  of
unlimited bodily forms one injures and degrades  their  eternal  self.  This
understanding saves one from acquiring <i>karma</i> or reactions to  actions  which
is the main cause of confinement to <i>samsara</i> or the perpetual cycle of  birth
and death. By gaining this basic realisation one achieves the goal of  <i>atma-</i>
<i>tattva</i> or soul realisation and then becomes eligible  for  <i>param  gatim</i>  the
supreme destination and highest attainment.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Lord Krishna now presents the results of the afore stated realisations  that
of perceiving the Supreme Lord present within  all  bodies  demigod,  human,
animal, etc. and His imperishable nature present everywhere. Such a <i>jiva</i>  or
embodied being <i>na hinasti</i> never degrades  <i>atmanam</i>  their  immortal  soul  by
being attached to material  desire  and  prolonging  their  imprisonment  in
material existence. The essence is one  who  sees  created  beings  separate
from the Supreme Lord and separate from  themselves  will  be  compelled  to
revolve in <i>samsara</i> the perpetual cycle of birth and death  in  the  material
existence and continue to perform sinful and degraded activities that  keeps
them shackled permanently in the  prison  of  material  existence.  This  is
corroborated by Sakuntala's statement in Moksa Dharma that: Such a  one  who
has not realised the <i>atma</i> or immortal soul inherent within all beings;  what
sinful activity did such a cheater of themselves deign not  to  commit?  The
Isa Upanisad, verse  III  reveals  the  resultant  consequence  due  to  the
greatest ignorance for those that degrade their own <i>atma</i>  and  the  soul  of
others living entities by engaging in  evil  activities  of  unrighteousness
beginning: <i>asurya nama te loka andhena</i> meaning such sinful demons  oblivious
of their inherent divine nature enter the dark, gloomy hellish planets  when
their life has ended.
<p>
