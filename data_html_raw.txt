<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Furthermore Lord Krishna explains that His manifestation  as  <i>Parabrahma</i>  is
the source of the <i>brahman</i> or spiritual substratum  pervading  all  existence
and is within and without all living entities  both  moving  and  stationary
and all that is animate as well as inanimate. This is so  even  as  gold  is
exists in a bracelet, a necklace and a ring equally  or  that  water  exists
within unlimited waves and countless drops of rain. <i>Parabrahma</i> alone is  the
sole cause of everything, all effects in inumerable  and  variegated  forms;
yet  so  subtle  that  it  has  no  material  form  or  qualities.   It   is
incomprehensible and inconceivable being incapable to be understood  by  the
mind as  well  imperceptible  to  the  senses.  Therefore  to  the  ignorant
<i>Parabrahma</i> is very far as millions of miles away  being  transcendental  and
far  beyond  <i>prakriti</i>  or  the  material   substratum   pervading   physical
existence. Yet to the spiritually intelligent it  very  close  and  near  as
within one's own heart in its manifestation as the <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul.
The Iso Upanisad V beginning <i>tad  ejati  tan  naijati  tad  dure  tadvantike</i>
means: The Supreme Lord moves and moves not; He is far away and  very  near;
He is within and without. <i>Ejati</i> means move, <i>naijati</i>  means  moves  not.  The
words <i>tadvantike</i> should be divided as  <i>tat</i>  meaning  He  the  Supreme  Lord,
<i>v</i> meaning also and <i>antike</i> meaning very near. Thus it  is  clearly  confirmed
that Supreme Lord is always present.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
The word <i>bahih</i> means outside, this refers  to  the  <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul
which still exists even when  it  departs  a  physical  body  at  death  and
abandons the subtle body along with  the  five  elements  of  earth,  water,
fire, air and ether  although  it  is  present  within  them  as  well.   As
confirmed  in  the  Chandogya  Upanisad  VI.VII.VII  beginning  <i>sa  ya  esho</i>
<i>animaitad</i>  means:  The  source  of  the  <i>brahman</i>  or  spiritual   substratum
pervading all existence is  <i>Parabrahma</i>  the  Supreme  Lord  Krishna  who  is
eternally existing in everything by His transcendental  potency.  By  nature
it is <i>acaram</i> or stationary and unmoving but it is <i>caram</i>  or  moving  in  its
embodied  state.  Due  to  being  supra-subtle  without  material,   mundane
qualities it is <i>avijneyam</i> or incomprehensible. The  <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul
possesses all powers and knowledge  although  it  occupies  and  inhabits  a
bodily  form.  Because  it  is  subatomic  it  can  not  be   perceived   by
technological methods and remains  a  mystery.  Therefore  it  is  extremely
difficult for worldly people of logic and  reason  to  understand  it  being
separate and distinct from the physical body. The  words  <i>durastham  cantike</i>
<i>ca tat</i> means that the <i>atma</i> although present within their own bodies is  very
far away from those who are arrogant, ostentatious, irreverent, etc. who  do
not possesses the 20 virtues of righteousness given in verses 8 to  12  such
as humility, reverence and nonviolence. But contrarily it is extremely  near
to those dear ones who possess the 20 virtues.  This  is  because  these  20
virtues facilitate a living entities  ability  to  perceive  internally  and
realize the <i>atma.</i> 
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The Supreme Lord Krishna who is known as <i>Parabrahma</i> is  the  source  of  the
<i>brahman</i> or spiritual substratum pervading all  existence  which  is  without
and within the elements such as earth, water, fire, air,  ether,  etc.;  yet
He is outside of them  and  distinctly  different  and  separate  from  them
because He is their cause and they are His products. At the same time He  is
within all as well constituted as physical bodies constituted  by  the  five
elements in the inanimate  immovable  and  the  animate  movable.  In  every
aspect of creation He is existing in a subatomic manifestation in all  <i>jivas</i>
or embodied beings of every living entity as  the  <i>atma</i>  or  immortal  soul.
Because He exists in an extremely subtle subatomic state  it  is  impossible
to perceive Him by the senses or understand  Him  by  the  mind.  So  He  is
acutely incomprehensible and it is very difficult for  transmigrating  <i>jivas</i>
to realise that  He  is  different  and  separate  from  the  physical  body
although He resides within the etheric heart of all <i>jivas</i> as the  <i>atma.</i>  For
those <i>jivas</i> who are bereft of the  20  virtues  given  by  Lord  Krishna  in
verses 8 to 12 the <i>atma</i> is very far away indeed  meaning  the  consciousness
required for self-realisation in order to  realise  the  <i>atma</i>  is  very  far
away. Those <i>jivas</i> who are endowed with these 20 virtues  such  as  humility,
non-violence, serving the Vaisnava spiritual master, etc.; the <i>atma</i> is  very
near indeed meaning that the consciousness required for self-realisation  in
order to to realise the <i>atma</i> is very near.
<p>
