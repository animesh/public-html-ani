<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
The renunciation of actions factually means not being attached to  them.  It
does not mean actually giving  them  up,  as  that  is  impossible.   Nobody
neither a wise man or a  fool  under  any  circumstances  can  refrain  from
actions for a moment. The reason Lord Krishna is stating  is  that  all  are
compelled to act forced by one's likes  and  dislikes  influenced  by  one's
attachments and aversions arising  out  of  <i>prakriti,</i>  material  nature  and
designated by one's past life activities.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
That it is impossible to renounce every single action one  makes  is  what's
being clarified here
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Now begins the summation.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Actions are spoken of as two-fold in the Paingi scripture. Actions that  are
dependent upon a cause and and actions which  are independent of any  cause.
The actions that are dependent upon a cause are  due  to  the  influence  of
<i>prakriti,</i> material nature. Independent actions  are  only  manifesting  from
the Supreme Lord. Therefore the words <i>karyate he  avasah</i>  means  to  perform
activities fully dependent, this dependence is on the Supreme Lord  Krishna.
The scriptures  also  further  clarify  that  <i>ah</i>  means  the  all  pervasive
Brahman.
<p>
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
No living being in the material existence can remain  inactive  even  for  a
moment without initiating or pursuing  some  activity  or  another.  If  one
should steadfastly determine to perform absolutely nothing, still one  would
be compelled into action by the qualities of   the  the  three  <i>gunas</i>  being
<i>sattva</i> or goodness, <i>rajas</i> or  passion  and  <i>tamas</i>  or  ignorance,  all  from
<i>prakriti,</i> material nature. The effects of these have sprung  into  existence
from one's past life activities. Thus by adhering to <i>karma yoga</i> the yoga  of
actions without desires one's accumulated sins will gradually  dissolve  and
then mastery over the <i>gunas</i> and <i>prakriti</i>  is  achieved.  At  this  time  the
mind has become pure and one becomes qualified for <i>jnana yoga.</i> Otherwise  as
Lord Krishna states anyone attempting  to pursue <i>jnana yoga</i> in lieu of  this
is a charlatan and all their attempts is merely a charade.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
It should not be speculated that one is entitled to the fruits
of one’s actions simply  because  it  is  impossibe  to  fully
renounce all actions. To  empasise  this  point  Lord  Krishna
states vimudh-atma meaning one ignorant of the soul. A fool or
a wise person can appear inactive without engaging in  worldly
affairs or  Vedic  activities.  So  the  sense  here  is  that
prakriti, material nature is propelling one to  act  based  on
the effects of one’s past actions which are  attached  to  the
root of the physical and subtle body. Therefore the conclusion
is that one should first purify themselves by performing karma
yoga which is actions without desires  as  prescribed  in  the
Vedas which destroys sins which hinder  spiritual  development
before embarking on the path of jnana yoga the cultivation  of
spiritual knowledge.
<p>
</div></td></tr></table><center>Thus ends commentaries of chapter 3, verse 5 of the Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.</center>
