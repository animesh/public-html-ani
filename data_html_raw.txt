<br><div align=center><h2>Commentaries of the Four Authorized Vaisnava Sampradayas</h2><b>as confirmed in the Garga Samhita Canto 10, Chapter 61, Verses 23, 24, 25, 26</b></div><table width=100% cellspacing=16 cellpadding=6><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Rudra Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic06-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Visnuswami</font></td></tr></table><center>Sridhara Swami's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
Of trees Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i> or divine, transcendental  opulence  is  the
<i>Asvtthah</i> or sacred Banyan tree.  Of  celestial  sages  being  those  who  as
demigods became proficient in singing Vedic hymns  and  promulgating  <i>bhakti</i>
or exclusive loving devotion to the Supreme Lord,  His  <i>vibhuti</i>  is  Narada.
And among those  who  from  their  very  birth  were  knowledgeable  of  the
Ultimate Truth and perfected beings, His  <i>vibhuti</i>  is  the  saint  known  as
Kapiladeva the son of Devahuti.
<p>
<p>
<p>
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Brahma Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic05-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Madhvacarya</font></td></tr></table><center>Madhvacarya's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Lord Krishna states the word <i>siddhanam</i>  meaning  of  perfected  beings,  His
<i>vibhuti</i>  is  <i>Kapila</i>   the  son  of  Devahuti.  The  name  Kapila   is   very
interesting. <i>Ka</i> means of wonderful form, <i>pi</i> means to nuture and <i>la</i> means  to
absorb the worlds. The words <i>priti</i> or love, <i>sukham</i> or  pleasure  and  <i>ananda</i>
or  bliss  are  corresponding  words.  The  <i>avatar</i>  Kapila  having  complete
awareness knows past,  present  and  future.  In  the  Bavravya  section  is
stated: Kapiladeva is aware of all that was born  previously,  all  that  is
being born now and all that will be born subsequently. Through  immeasurable
pleasure, through nourishment, through bliss and through Kapiladeva  can  be
known.
<p>
</div></td></tr><tr><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Sri Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic07-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Ramanuja</font></td></tr></table><center>Ramanuja's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
<p>
Of trees Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i> or divine, transcendental  opulence  is  the
sacred Asvattha worshippable by everyone. Of elevated sages His  <i>vibhuti</i>  is
Narada because of  his  phenomenal  level  of  <i>bhakti</i>  or  exclusive  loving
devotion unto the Supreme Lord.
<p>
</div></td><td style="border: 1px solid" bgcolor=#404080 valign=top width=50%><div align=center><u>Kumara Vaisnava Sampradaya:</u></div><p><table align=left hspace=20 vspace=8 ><tr><td align=center><img hspace=8 src="/DiscipleSuccession/pics/icons/pic08-2.jpg" width=60><br><font size=1>Nimbaditya</font></td></tr></table><center>Kesava Kasmiri's Commentary</center><p><div align=justify><p>
Among trees Lord Krishna's <i>vibhuti</i> or  divine,  transcendental  opulence  is
the <i>Asvattha</i> or the sacred Banyan tree also called the  holy  fig  tree  and
known as Ficus Bengalensis. Among celestial sages His <i>vibhuti</i> is  Narada  of
exceptional <i>bhakti</i>  or  loving  devotion.  Of  the  <i>Gandarvas</i>  or  celestial
singers and musicians His vibhuti is Citraratha of outstanding  singing.  Of
perfected  beings  who  have  since  inception  acquired  spiritual  wisdom,
dispassion, renunciation etc. and are endowed with  great  purity,  holiness
and  enlightenment  possessing  all  <i>siddhis</i>  or  supernatural  powers,  His
<i>vibhuti</i> is Kapiladeva the son of Kardama Muni and Devahuti.
<p>
